## ANVISA, PMDA and MFDS

- **ANVISA (Brazil):** GMP certificates were published in Brazil's official gazette (DOU):
  - Pithampur: Jul-23, May-24 (hormones) and Jul-25.
  - Rau: Aug-25, its first certificate.

  None of them is backed by a recorded site visit; they look like desk or reliance reviews.
- **PMDA (Japan):** only Rau holds Japan's foreign-manufacturer accreditation (AG12300303), renewed 27 Feb 2022. This review is document-based.
- **MFDS (Korea):** the company claims an approval for Pithampur but gives no date. Korean drug master file (KDMF) filings exist for both sites.

The company says ANVISA and PMDA inspected the sites in FY23–FY26. Those audits most likely account for the 6 unattributed audits: 2 in FY23 and 4 in FY25.

## Adverse events not disclosed in the IPO documents

1. **US FDA, Pithampur, 1–5 Jul 2019: OAI.** The OAI classification comes from FDA dashboard data via two aggregators. The Form 483 has 4 observations, including about 21,372 aborted chromatography runs. No warning letter or import alert was found.
2. **EU GMP non-compliance, Rau sterile operations.** Italy's AIFA relayed the statement on 8 Apr 2020. Non-sterile APIs were re-certified in Jul 2020.

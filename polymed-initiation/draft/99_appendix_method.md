## How to read this report {#how-to-read}

**Scope.** This is an initiating-coverage report on the business and the industry. It has no earnings estimates, valuation, rating or target price. Where management has given guidance, it is reported as management's target and attributed to the call or presentation in which it was given. <!-- lint-ok -->

**Every number is traceable.**
- Each figure is drawn from a fact ledger built from company filings, presentations, call transcripts, rating-agency rationales, regulator texts and peer offer documents.
- Hover over any number, or press on it on a touch screen, to see the source document, its date and the page or slide.
- Figures computed by us (shares, growth rates, CAGRs, currency conversions) are labelled "our calculation", and the tooltip shows the formula and its inputs.
- Market-research estimates from commercial vendors are labelled as such and used only as ranges.

**Conventions.**
- Currency: rupee amounts are in Rs. mn and Rs. bn; company figures reported in crore or lakh are converted. <!-- lint-ok -->
- Periods: fiscal years end on 31 March (FY26 = April 2025 to March 2026) and quarters are written 1QFY27. European and other overseas subsidiaries report on calendar years (CY25).
- Growth is year-on-year (yoy) unless stated. <!-- lint-ok -->
- Geography: "West Asia" refers to the region the company calls the Middle East. <!-- lint-ok -->
- "Standalone" is Poly Medicure Ltd, the Indian listed entity. "Consolidated" adds the subsidiaries and the equity-accounted associate.

**What we do not know.**
- **Presentations.** They disclose revenue for five therapy lines: infusion, renal, cardiology, orthopaedics and "others".
- **The BRSR.** The statutory Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report adds the share of standalone turnover for:
  - blood management and collection;
  - surgery and wound drainage;
  - urology;
  - anaesthesia and respiratory, in FY25;
  - prefilled flush syringes.

  We convert these shares to rupees as our calculation.
- **Not disclosed.** Diagnostics, gastroenterology and oncology are not broken out, and nothing is split at the consolidated level.
- **Our approach.** We do not estimate splits beyond these disclosures. Where a line's size matters to the argument, we say it is not disclosed and list the question in the plant-visit question bank.

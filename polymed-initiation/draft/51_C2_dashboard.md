## C2 Quarterly monitoring dashboard {#c2}

Each KPI below tests a driver from Section A7 or a risk from Section C1. The latest datapoint is the most recent disclosed figure; the "signal" column states what would change our reading of the business. Most items come from the quarterly presentation and call. Related-party, workforce and subsidiary data come once a year, with the annual report and BRSR in August.

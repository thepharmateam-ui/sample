### How to use this bank

1. **Short meeting of about an hour.** Work through the must-ask list below, in order. It covers strategy, the FY26 reset, the four core lines, customers, competition, policy, the plant, margins and governance.
2. **Full plant visit.** Add the "if time permits" questions for the business-line and plant heads you meet. They are grouped by theme in report order.
3. **On the floor.** Use the observation checklist at the end to test what you are told against what you see.
4. **Afterwards.** Compare the answers with the context line on each card, and note any answer that changes a driver in Section A7 of the report.

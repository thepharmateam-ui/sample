## A3 The FY26 reset and the guidance record {#a3}

FY26 broke a run of on-guidance delivery.
- **Headline growth.** Consolidated revenue grew {{CA-0107}} to {{A-0039}}, against the {{CA-0512}} management guided in May-2025. Standalone revenue grew only {{CA-0093|approx}} ([[EXREF:q_growth]]).
- **Organic growth.** Excluding the part-year contribution of PendraCare and Citieffe, consolidated revenue grew {{D-M-003}} (our calculation on the company's organic figure of {{CB-0577}}).
- **Both halves missed, exports by more.** Domestic revenue grew {{A-0265}} (vs. {{CA-0513|range}} guided). International revenue grew {{A-0276}} including acquisitions (vs. {{CA-0516|range}} guided) but was 'flattish' on an organic basis, and management put 4QFY26 standalone exports at minus {{CA-0164|range}}. <!-- TR4Q25 p.5; TR3Q26 p.16; TR4Q26 p.12 -->

The contrast with FY25 frames the debate. That year standalone revenue grew {{CA-0491|approx}} against {{CA-0690|range}} guided, and management said it had 'very accurately' forecast revenue; consolidated revenue grew {{CA-0485|approx}}, just below the range. <!-- TR4Q25 p.3 -->

Two readings of FY26 are possible:
1. an external shock layered on a destocking cycle in the export channel;
2. evidence that the export franchise is more exposed to Chinese pricing, US tariffs and regulatory friction than the FY21-25 record suggested.

We read the 1QFY27 print as supporting the first reading more than the second. Standalone growth recovered ([[EXREF:q_growth]]), and Europe grew {{CB-0324}} organically on new customers. We nonetheless see three company-specific elements that matter for FY27-30:
1. European certification and tender slippage;
2. price competition in renal from Chinese dialysers that, management says, enter India duty-free via ASEAN countries; <!-- TR4Q26 p.8; TR1Q27S p.9 -->
3. a guidance practice that moved targets without always acknowledging it.

### What happened, quarter by quarter

The reset ran in three phases:
1. **Southern Europe slowed in 4QFY25.** Management linked this to 'supply chain disruption, maybe overstocking' and uncertainty over US tariffs. <!-- TR4Q25 p.8 -->
2. **Europe contracted in 1HFY26:** {{CB-2038}} yoy in 1QFY26 and {{CB-1781}} in 2QFY26. Management gave these reasons: <!-- TR1Q26 p.5, p.9, p.14; TR2Q26 p.4, p.9, p.11 -->
   - distributors cut inventory from {{WC-0068|range}} months of cover to {{WC-0069|range}} months;
   - the reopening of the Suez route shortened transit from about {{WRA-0002}} to about {{WRA-0003|num}} month, releasing pipeline stock;
   - Chinese product was redirected from the US into Europe;
   - EU MDR certificate renewals came through only in Apr-Jun 2025.
3. **Recovery from 3QFY26,** helped by the consolidation of the acquisitions. Europe grew {{CB-1491}} in 3QFY26 and {{CB-0838}} in 4QFY26 as reported. Management also cited National Health Service contracts in the UK delayed by {{CA-0290|range}} years after the change of government, and products dropped during the MDR transition. <!-- TR3Q26 p.8, p.16 -->

In India, private-sector revenue grew {{CA-0419}} and {{CA-0332}} in 1QFY26 and 2QFY26, while government business fell {{CA-0420|abs}} and {{CA-0333|abs}}. Management also pointed to a one-off inventory realignment at distributors around the September 2025 GST rate cut. <!-- TR2Q26 p.5, p.8 -->

### The guidance record

Management gives explicit annual guidance and revisits it every quarter, so the record is easy to score.
- **FY22-FY25:** revenue guidance was met or beaten every year on management's own (for FY24-FY25, standalone) scoring. The growth was built on a mix that routinely missed: domestic revenue fell short of guidance in FY23, FY24 and FY25, renal revenue missed in every year from FY22 to FY24, and US revenue targets slipped by years. <!-- TR4Q22 p.9; TR4Q23 p.2; TR3Q24b p.7-8; TR4Q24 p.4, p.7 -->
- **Management's method:** it describes starting 'with a moderate number' and revising after the first or second quarter. <!-- TR3Q24b p.7 -->
- **FY26:**
  - Revenue guidance was cut from {{CA-0512}} to {{CA-0364|range}} in Nov-2025, then missed.
  - Renal and domestic guidance were missed by a wide margin.
  - 2HFY26 revenue was guided at {{WM-0067|range}} above 1HFY26 in Nov-2025, restated in Feb-2026 as ~{{CA-0251|range}} ('as I mentioned in the previous call'), and described in May-2026 as 'exactly delivered' at ~{{CA-0112}}. <!-- TR2Q26 p.6; TR3Q26 p.6; TR4Q26 p.5 -->
- **FY27 standalone:** the revenue guidance was widened in Aug-2026 while being described as maintained ([[EXREF:fy27_guide]]). <!-- TR4Q26 p.6; TR1Q27S p.4 -->

We read these as signs that guidance is framed as a target to be managed toward rather than a forecast with a stated error band, so we weight management's quarterly commentary on drivers more heavily than the point guidance.

**Vision 2030** ('PolyMed 3.0'): double revenue by FY30. Management characterized the current business as {{CA-0007|plain}}, and expects a similar split. An analyst on the same call worked this out as about {{WM-0065|range}} a year organic and {{WM-0066|range}} from acquisitions, which the MD described as 'a trajectory basically'. <!-- TR1Q27S p.2, p.7 -->

The FY27 plan rests on four things:
1. a recovery in Europe that 1QFY27 has begun to show;
2. a normalization of West Asia shipping, which 1QFY27 has not;
3. renal growth despite Chinese price pressure, which management is contesting through an anti-dumping petition;
4. a full-year contribution from the acquired businesses, whose margins management puts below the group's.

::: callout title="What would change our read" kind="debate"
Evidence that FY26 was structural rather than cyclical would include three things:
1. Organic export growth staying in single digits once West Asia logistics normalize.
2. Renal revenue declining for a second year without an anti-dumping duty.
3. Receivable days staying above the FY26 level, which management attributed to 'better terms' extended to international customers. <!-- TR4Q26 p.12 -->

Evidence that it was cyclical would be:
- organic Europe growth holding in the high teens through FY27;
- renal volumes recovering after a DGTR (Directorate General of Trade Remedies) determination.
:::

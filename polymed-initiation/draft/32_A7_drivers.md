## A7 What drives growth and margins: the driver maps {#a7}

This section pulls together the company-specific drivers from the business-line chapters in Section A4. Each driver is labelled by its origin:
- **Management target:** a figure or ambition management has stated.
- **Our view:** our reading of the evidence, with the evidence shown.

Neither is a forecast. The ranking reflects how much each driver can move group revenue or margin over the medium term, on the disclosed evidence.

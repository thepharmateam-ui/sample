## C3 Plant-visit question bank {#c3}

This chapter turns the open issues in this report into questions for a meeting with management during a plant visit. Each question card gives:
- why the question matters, meaning the driver or disclosure gap it tests;
- one line of context from our research;
- what a reassuring or a concerning answer sounds like;
- a follow-up.

It has three layers:
- **The must-ask list** (below) is for a short meeting of about an hour.
- **The full bank** is for a day-long visit with time for business-line heads, grouped by theme in the same order as the report.
- **The floor-walk checklist** at the end lists things to observe rather than ask.

::: callout title="Compliance: ask about structure, not the current quarter" kind="note"
Listed-company managements cannot share unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI) under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.
- **What the questions do.** They ask about business structure, strategy, economics and data the company has already disclosed.
- **What they avoid.** Current-quarter trading, order-book numbers, unannounced transactions and changes to guidance.
- **If an answer strays into UPSI**, stop the line of questioning and note it.
- **Numbers in the context lines** come from public filings, presentations and call transcripts cited in this report.
:::

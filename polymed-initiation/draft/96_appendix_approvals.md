- **Reconciling the two.** The May-2026 deck splits the company's count into {{CB-1095}} for Polymed, {{CB-1097}} for Citieffe and {{CB-1099}} for Plan1Health, and says only that PendraCare's guiding catheter is approved. <!-- DK4Q26 p.38 --> Polymed's and Citieffe's figures equal their Traditional submissions in the table, so we read the company's count as leaving out Special 510(k)s and PendraCare's clearances. <!-- FDA510K_POLY p.1; FDA510K_CIT p.1; DK4Q26 p.38 -->
- **Recent additions.** Since Apr 2025 the group has added the Primum guiding catheter, the Estremo fibular nail, Special 510(k)s for the Polyshield catheter and the Polywin Safety size range, and PendraCare's SimplGuide introducer. <!-- FDA510K_POLY p.1; FDA510K_CIT p.1; FDA510K_PEN p.1 -->

### European Union: MDR and CE marking

Europe produced {{CB-0012}} of FY26 revenue, and a device sold there needs a CE mark. <!-- DK1Q27 p.4; PD21 p.38 --> Depending on its risk class, the maker must involve an independent notified body before it can affix the mark. When the Medical Device Regulation, EU Regulation 2017/745, took effect on 26 May 2021, the number of designated notified bodies was very low, which the European Commission says created bottlenecks in pre-market certification. <!-- EU_COM2025_1023 p.1 --> Management said in Feb 2026 that Polymed "had to drop many products initially in the bargain because it was taking a long time to get the approvals". <!-- TR3Q26 p.8 -->

The transition rules set the deadline for any directive-era certificates still in use:
- **Transition.** Under EU Regulation 2023/607, devices certified under the old directives may stay on the market until 31 Dec 2027 for class III and implantable class IIb devices and until 31 Dec 2028 for other classes, provided the maker had applied for MDR certification by 26 May 2024 and signed with a notified body by 26 Sep 2024. <!-- EU_REG2023_607 p.4 -->
- **Revision.** The Commission proposed a targeted revision of the MDR in Dec 2025, and the European Parliament's health committee began work on it on 14 Jul 2026. <!-- EU_COM2025_1023 p.1; DSV_MDR_2607 p.1 -->
- **The acquired makers.** Citieffe says its whole portfolio is already certified under MDR, while PendraCare's products carry a mix of directive and MDR approvals; PendraCare's acquisition call described EU approvals as "extremely challenging" to obtain under MDR. <!-- TRCIT p.4; TRPEN p.3 -->

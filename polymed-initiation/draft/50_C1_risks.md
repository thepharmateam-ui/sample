### The three risks we weigh most

1. **Chinese pricing, in infusion and in renal.** This is the risk most likely to change the margin structure.
   - In infusion, management credits new customers for the 1QFY27 European recovery ({{CB-0324}} organic growth), which we read as evidence that the FY26 dip was largely cyclical. <!-- DK1Q27 p.26; TR1Q27S p.3 -->
   - In renal, the company has chosen price over volume; we see renal revenue growth as dependent on the outcome of the anti-dumping case. <!-- TR1Q27S pp.3-4 -->
2. **Execution on two acquisitions and three plants at once.** The management layer is being built while integration and construction run in parallel. The early numbers for PendraCare are weak. <!-- AR26 p.122 -->
3. **Indian pricing policy.** A cap on trade margins would compress MRP-based pricing in the domestic trade channel. Its effect depends on the channel mix, which Polymed does not disclose (Section B3).

## Board and key management {#people}

At 31 Mar 2026 Polymed's board had {{WM-0051}} directors: {{D-WE-003}} executive (our calculation) and {{WE-0001}} non-executive, of whom {{WE-0002}} were independent. <!-- AR26 p.51 --> The Aug-2024 placement document had shown {{D-0326}} directors, {{D-0327}} of them independent. <!-- PD24 p.183 --> The changes since then:
- **Independent terms ended.** Dr Shailendra Raj Mehta and Dr Sandeep Bhargava left on completing their terms on 26 Sep 2024. <!-- AR25 p.38 -->
- **The chairman was reclassified.** D.R. Mehta's tenure as an independent director ended at the 29th AGM in Sep 2024, and shareholders re-appointed him as a non-executive, non-independent director. <!-- AR24 p.14; AR26 p.53 --> The 31st AGM re-appointed him by special resolution, which is required because he is over {{WM-0055}} old; {{WM-0039}} of institutional votes were cast against. <!-- AGMN26 p.1; PBN26 p.6 -->
- **One independent joined.** Vishal Gupta joined on 28 Oct 2024. <!-- AR26 p.52 -->
- **Executive seats were added.** Mukulika Baid, a promoter-family non-executive director, stepped down on 6 May 2025, the day Vishal Baid became an executive director; Pankaj Kumar Gupta, the head of operations, followed on 8 Aug 2025. <!-- AR26 p.53, p.88 -->

## A4.5 Diagnostics (blood collection) {#a4-5}

Diagnostics at Polymed means venous blood collection (Haemochek vacuum tubes, needles, holders and a safety butterfly set), sold mainly in India to laboratories and hospitals. Management pushed the line in 2021-2022 and opened a dedicated plant in FY24, but has not mentioned it on a results call since Oct-2024; its revenue, undisclosed since FY22, sits inside the BRSR's "Blood Management & Blood Collection" family with blood bags (Section A4.4). <!-- CATDOM25 p.30; TR4Q22 p.15; TR1Q22 p.4; TR3Q22 p.4; TR1Q24 p.9; TR2Q25 p.6; BRSR26 p.12 -->

### Products and the unit of demand

Haemochek tubes are PET and colour-coded by additive, and so by test: serum and gel-separator tubes for chemistry, citrate for coagulation, K2 or K3 EDTA for blood counts, heparin for plasma chemistry, fluoride for glucose, plus ESR and CPDA (blood-grouping) tubes. A multi-sample needle (20G to 23G, including the HaemoFlash flashback version) in a standard holder fills several tubes from one venepuncture; the Nuovo Safe winged set, with a locking needle shield, is for small or difficult veins. <!-- CATDOM25 p.30; CATEXP24 pp.61-65; WEB_DIAGNOSTICS p.1 -->

Demand is one needle per venepuncture and a tube per group of tests, so volumes follow laboratory test counts. The catalogues list no lancets; the heparinized blood-gas syringe ({{D-0865}} in the 3 ml size) is sold with infusion products, and the Aug-2025 deck still shows a VTM kit under diagnostics. <!-- D_product_glossary A4.5; CATDOM25 p.34; DK1Q26 p.28 -->

### How the line was built, and whether Polymed makes the tubes

- **Early range.** Tubes, needles, safety sets and a holder were in the FY16 product list. The Oct-2019 deck, the only one to split the line out, put diagnostics at {{CC-1081}} of sales (blood transfusion {{CC-1080}}). <!-- AR16 p.6; DK2019 p.13 -->
- **COVID.** When tubes ran short, laboratories reliant on imports turned to Polymed, which "had the production of these items" (MD, Jul-2021). VTM kits and respiratory products added {{CC-0306|range}} to 1QFY22 domestic sales, demand that was gone by FY23. <!-- TR1Q22 p.4; TR1Q23 p.5 -->
- **The 2021-2022 push.** A diagnostics head (Nov-2021), dedicated distributors and new test kits were meant to "fill the same pipe" into laboratories already buying Polymed's tubes, with equipment to follow. <!-- TR2Q22 p.3-4; TR3Q22 p.8, p.11; TR2021 p.12 -->
- **PLI.** By Feb-2022 Polymed had approval under the IVD category of the PLI scheme for pharmaceuticals (renal is in the separate medical-devices scheme): {{CC-0176}} of incremental sales from FY23, for a committed {{CC-0917}} of investment over FY22-FY28. Approved products are blood-collection tubes, viral transport media, rapid tests (HIV, hepatitis, malaria, dengue, typhoid) and an RNA extraction kit; the FY22 annual report counts {{WG-0005}} IVD applicants among {{WG-0004}} selected. <!-- TR3Q22 p.4; TR4Q22 p.3; DKMAY22 p.18; DOP_PPO24 p.17; AR22 p.46 -->
- **Plant.** The first of four new plants, in Faridabad, started production in 1QFY24; management confirmed it was for diagnostics. <!-- TR2Q23 p.12; TR1Q24 p.9; TR4Q24 p.3 -->

**Made or bought.** The Feb-2021 placement document lists tubes among products "manufactured", yet the Aug-2024 one names blood-collection tubes first among traded goods in FY22-FY24. FY22 traded sales ({{WB-0037}}, including dialysis machines) were below even the lowest FY22 diagnostics figure management gave ({{CC-0215|range}}), so part of the range was already made in-house (our inference). Since the new plant opened, traded sales have roughly halved ([[EXREF:dx_traded]]). We read this as the remaining bought-in tubes moving in-house from FY24, though the company has not named its later traded items. <!-- PD21 p.131; PD24 p.107, p.110; TR3Q22 p.20; AR26 p.113 -->

### Market and customers

**Demand follows test volumes, which are low in India.** Frost & Sullivan (Oct-2025, in the Integris Medtech prospectus) puts yearly diagnostic tests per capita at {{WG-0007}} in India and China against {{WG-0008}} in the US, and sees standalone laboratories giving way to chains, citing revenue CAGRs of {{WG-0009}} for Dr. Lal PathLabs (2019-2024) and {{WG-0010}} for Metropolis (2015-2024). CRISIL (commissioned by Polymed) sizes India's IVD and reagents segment at {{D-0430}} in FY24, growing {{D-0432}} a year to FY28. We found no size for India's tube market. <!-- DRHP_INTEG p.241; PD24 p.137 -->

**Customers.** In May-2021 the MD named Thyrocare, Metropolis, Dr. Lal PathLabs, SRL and Pathkind among its laboratory customers, adding Vijay Diagnostics in Feb-2022. Prices and customer concentration are [not disclosed]. <!-- TR2021 p.14; TR3Q22 p.14 -->

**Global.** 360iResearch projects the tube market at {{E-0364}} in 2026 and {{WG-0013}} by 2032, a {{WG-0012}} CAGR (vendor estimate). Filed numbers grow more slowly: BD's Specimen Management unit (Vacutainer tubes, needles, wingsets, blood-gas devices) sold {{E-0202}} in FY25, up {{WG-0011}} despite a decline in China. <!-- VND_RNM_BCT p.1; BD_10K25 p.1 -->

### Competition

- **BD.** Polymed's MD calls vacuum tubes "vacutainers". BD has an FDA warning letter open on Specimen Management and from Oct-2025 reports it in a new Medical Essentials segment with its infusion consumables, which we read as treating blood collection as a consumables business, as Polymed does. <!-- TR3Q22 p.14; BD_10K25 p.1 -->
- **Other multinationals.** Nipro sells Neotube vacuum tubes; Greiner Bio-One, Sarstedt and Terumo are among the suppliers 360iResearch profiles, with no filed data on their tube sales or Indian presence. <!-- NIPRO_FY25 p.22; VND_RNM_BCT p.1 -->
- **HMD** (Hindustan Syringes & Medical Devices), also in Faridabad, sells its Vaku range of evacuated tubes, needles, winged sets and holders, developed with Israel's Estar Technology; Dispovan is its syringe brand. <!-- HMD_WEB p.1 -->
- **Chinese makers.** KDL won every category of vacuum tubes and blood-collection needles in a Liaoning-led provincial alliance tender in CY25, and CE-registered venous blood-collection containers among {{WA-0016}} products. Weigao's blood-management line, which bundles collection with storage and separation, sold {{E-0270}} in CY25. <!-- KDL_AR25 p.21; WEIGAO_AR25 p.17; WEIGAO_IR26 p.21 -->

Against them Polymed offers local manufacture, public-procurement preference and a full collection basket, but claims no market share. <!-- DOP_PPO24 p.17; CATDOM25 p.30 -->

### Regulation

- **India.** Class A and B devices have needed licences under the Medical Devices Rules since 1 Oct 2022; management said in Aug-2022 that most of its devices are Class B. For incentives and procurement the tubes count as IVD products: the Department of Pharmaceuticals' Oct-2024 order deeming PLI makers Class-II local suppliers lists them under the pharmaceuticals scheme. <!-- PIB_MDRAB_2209 p.1; TR1Q23 p.8; DKMAY22 p.18; DOP_PPO24 p.1, p.17 -->
- **Exports.** Under EU-style rules tubes are IVD "specimen receptacles". Saudi Arabia's SFDA defines them as devices "whether of a vacuum-type or not" and puts them in its lowest IVD class (Class A), and the IVDR applies in Europe, where Polymed had no diagnostics business in May-2022. <!-- SFDA_REQ1 p.77, p.80; VND_RNM_BCT p.1; TR4Q22 p.15 -->

### Growth and margin drivers, ranked

1. **Test volumes and chain consolidation** (structural; our view). More draws, bought by chains Polymed already supplies. <!-- DRHP_INTEG p.241; TR2021 p.14 -->
2. **Cross-selling kits through the tube channel** (management strategy, 2021-2022). No result has been disclosed, and the MD conceded "there are many companies for diagnostic products". <!-- TR4Q22 p.15 -->
3. **Public procurement** (policy). Deemed local-supplier status helps in government laboratory tenders; its value is [not disclosed]. <!-- DOP_PPO24 p.1, p.17 -->
4. **Margin: make rather than buy** (our inference). Own output should earn more than traded tubes, and PLI adds {{CC-0176}} on eligible incremental sales; plant utilization is [not disclosed]. In May-2021 the MD expected margins similar to the core business. <!-- TR2021 p.14 -->
5. **Margin: price** (our view). Tubes are a commodity that Chinese makers win on price at home; import pricing is the main risk to the line's margin. <!-- KDL_AR25 p.21 -->

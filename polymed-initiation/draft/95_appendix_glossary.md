# Appendices {#appendices}

## Product glossary: what each device is and how it is used {#glossary}

This glossary lists every product type in Polymed's catalogues, website and placement documents, grouped by business line, with the brands under which the company sells them. It condenses the product map we built from the domestic, export and components catalogues, the company website, the cardiology ordering tables, the two placement documents, the FDA database and the acquisition presentations. <!-- CATDOM25 p.3; CATEXP24 p.3; CATCOMP24 p.4; WEB_HOME p.1; PD24 pp.173-174; FDA510K_POLY p.1; DKCIT p.12; DKPEN p.11 -->

::: callout title="How to read the tables" kind="note"
- **General descriptions.** The "What it is" and "Unit of demand" columns are general clinical descriptions written by us, not company disclosures. They explain what the device does and what drives its consumption, and they make no claim about Polymed.
- **Company data.** The "Polymed brands or range" column is company data; the documents behind it are listed under each table.
- **Sizes.** Gauge (G), French (F) and mL sizes are as printed in the catalogues.
- **Revenue.** Polymed does not disclose revenue by product type; Section A1 sets out what is disclosed.
:::

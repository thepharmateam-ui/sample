## C4 Channel checks to run {#c4}

Management interviews can be tested against people and data outside the company. The checks below map to the drivers and gaps in this report, interviews first and public data after; our priorities are set out below the table.

::: table id="channel_checks" title="Channel checks: distributors, dialysis chains, hospital buyers and public databases can test the main open questions"
| # | Who or what | What to ask or look for | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Medical-device distributors in Germany, Italy, Spain and the UK | Inventory cover now versus 2025; Polymed's price versus Chinese and Indian rivals; reasons for switching | Whether the FY26 dip was destocking or share loss (A3) |
| 2 | Dialysis chains (NephroPlus, NephroCare, Apex) and PMNDP PPP operators | Dialyser purchase prices by origin; single-use versus reuse practice; machine sourcing and service quality | Renal pricing, single-use conversion and machine pull-through (A4.3) |
| 3 | Hospital procurement heads at Indian corporate chains | Rate contracts, formulary status and trade-margin practice for cannulas, blood bags and urology products | India channel economics and exposure to a trade-margin cap (A5, B3) |
| 4 | Interventional cardiologists | Experience with Polymed stents and PendraCare catheters; price versus Meril, SMT and MNCs | Cardiology adoption (A4.8) |
| 5 | Orthopaedic surgeons and distributors in Italy and Mexico | Citieffe product preference; tender dynamics | Orthopaedics growth and pricing (A4.9) |
| 6 | Blood banks and NACO-linked buyers | Blood-bag sourcing, DEHP-free demand, tender prices | Blood-management line (A4.4) |
| 7 | Indian import data for dialysers (HS code 901839), by origin | Import volumes and unit values from China and Malaysia | Scale of the dumping problem (A4.3, B3) |
| 8 | US FDA databases: 510(k), registration and listing, recalls, Form 483s, import alerts | New clearances, listed OEM customers, inspection history | US readiness and quality (A5, B4) |
| 9 | EU tender portals (TED) and national tender sites | Awards to Polymed and to Chinese bidders; price per unit | European pricing and win rates (A4.1, B1) |
| 10 | Indian procurement portals (GeM, state medical corporations) | Rate contracts for cannulas, blood bags and dialysis consumables | Government pricing (B1) |
| 11 | Job postings and professional-network data | Hiring in automation, quality and regulatory roles; attrition at plants | Automation and labour model (A6) |
| 12 | Polymer and freight price indices | PVC, polypropylene and polycarbonate prices; container rates | Margin drivers (A2) |
| 13 | State minimum-wage notifications (Haryana, Rajasthan, Uttarakhand) | Wage revisions and effective dates | Labour cost (A2, A6) |
| 14 | Competitor filings (BD, B. Braun, Terumo, Nipro, Weigao, KDL, Indian peers) | Segment growth, pricing commentary and capacity moves in infusion and dialysis | Industry structure (B2) |
:::

We would prioritize the European distributor checks, the dialysis-chain checks and the dialyser import data: they test the two biggest swing factors, European exports and renal pricing, with evidence management cannot shape.

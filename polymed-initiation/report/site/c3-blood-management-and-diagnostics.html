Blood management and diagnostics

Q42If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

Blood management and blood collection is 9.5% of standalone turnover. How does that split between blood bags, collection tubes and needles, and who buys each?

Why it matters
The BRSR is the only disclosure of this line's size; the product split decides its growth and margin profile (Sections A4.4 and A4.5).
Our context
The BRSR share implies about Rs. 1.6bn of FY26 revenue (our calculation).
Listen for
Reassuring: growth in collection tubes and branded bags to private blood banks. Concerning: dependence on government blood-bank tenders.
Follow-up
What is your share of blood bags in India?

Q43If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

Where are you on DEHP-free blood bags, given the EU REACH sunset for DEHP in medical devices, and is there a price premium?

Why it matters
DEHP substitution is a regulatory transition that favours makers with validated alternatives (Section B5).
Our context
Polymed sold 965.3mn units of PVC-free products in FY25; DEHP's latest application date for medical devices in the EU is 1 January 2029.
Listen for
Reassuring: validated DEHP-free bags with European approvals. Concerning: no plan before the deadline.
Follow-up
Which plasticizer have you chosen, and who supplies it?

Q44If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

In vacuum blood-collection tubes, how do you compete with BD and Greiner in India, and is the line capacity-constrained?

Why it matters
Diagnostics is a high-volume line with global incumbents; the answer tests Polymed's right to win.
Our context
Revenue for tubes is [not disclosed]; they sit within the BRSR's blood-management line.
Listen for
Reassuring: private-lab contracts and a cost advantage. Concerning: presence only in low-end tenders.
Follow-up
Do you sell tubes and needles as a system to laboratories?

Q45If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

The BRSR put blood management and collection at 10.0% of FY25 standalone turnover, while the Aug-2024 placement document showed 5.9% of consolidated product sales for FY24. What moved into the line, and can you restate its history on one basis?

Why it matters
On our calculation the BRSR implies a line ~2.1x the size shown a year earlier, so every growth rate for blood depends on the basis (Section A4.4).
Our context
In Feb-2025 the MD described each category outside infusion and renal as under 5% of revenue.
Listen for
Reassuring: a named reclassification and a restated series. Concerning: no explanation for a step change of this size.
Follow-up
How did bags, administration sets and collection tubes each move in FY26, when the BRSR implies a -0.6% change for the line (our calculation)?

Q46If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

What share of blood-bag sales goes to government and charitable blood banks through tenders, to private blood banks and to exports, and how have tender prices moved over the past three years?

Why it matters
Public buying and incumbent capacity set prices: HLL Lifecare can make 12.5 mn pieces per annum, and Terumo Penpol made 30 mn bags per year in 2018 (Section A4.4).
Our context
Blood transfusion apparatus is one of 19 device lines reserved for Class-I local suppliers in public tenders, and the government's illustrative supplier list names Polymed.
Listen for
Reassuring: a rising private and export mix at stable tender prices. Concerning: dependence on state tenders at falling prices.
Follow-up
Do tenders now ask for BIS certification to ISO 3826, which HLL received first in Aug-2023, and do your bags hold it?

Q47If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

What is your blood-bag capacity today, in bag systems a year, and its utilization, and how much will Palwal add at Phase-1 (Mar-2027) and Phase-2 (Dec-2027)?

Why it matters
Management plans to double transfusion capacity but has never given a unit figure; utilization decides whether the new capacity helps or dilutes margins.
Our context
The MD said in Aug-2025 that transfusion capacity would double at Palwal, a plant he now describes as focused on orthopaedics and transfusion.
Listen for
Reassuring: a capacity number with tender or export demand to fill it. Concerning: capacity added ahead of visible demand.
Follow-up
Will Palwal bring PVC film extrusion or anticoagulant preparation in-house?

Q48If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

What share of the bag systems you sell are triple to quintuple, SAGM, top-and-bottom or leuko-filtered, and how does realization per system compare with single and double bags?

Why it matters
Component separation is the main structural lever for bag makers: lower-middle-income countries separate 81% of their blood against 98% in high-income countries (WHO).
Our context
Polymed's catalogues cover every configuration from single to quintuple systems, with in-line leuko-depletion filters and platelet bags.
Listen for
Reassuring: a rising share of multi-bag and filtered systems at a premium that holds in tenders. Concerning: sales concentrated in single and double bags.
Follow-up
What happened to the blood-bank equipment plans the MD outlined in Feb-2022?

Q49If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

Which blood-collection products are still bought in, and what are the capacity and utilization of the Faridabad diagnostics plant that started production in 1QFY24?

Why it matters
The Aug-2024 placement document named collection tubes among traded goods for FY22-FY24; own manufacture should carry a better margin (Section A4.5).
Our context
Consolidated traded-goods sales fell to Rs. 47mn in FY26, with the items not named. Under the IVD PLI Polymed committed Rs. 500mn of investment over FY22-FY28.
Listen for
Reassuring: tubes and needles made in-house, with a capacity figure. Concerning: continued reliance on bought-in tubes.
Follow-up
How much of the committed PLI investment has been spent?

Q50If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

What incentive has Polymed claimed or received under the IVD PLI for FY25 and FY26, and which products generate the eligible sales?

Why it matters
The incentive for FY24 is the only pointer to the line's scale since FY22: ~Rs. 14mn implies ~Rs. 280mn of eligible incremental sales (our calculation).
Our context
The scheme pays 5% on incremental sales of approved IVD products, which include blood-collection tubes, viral transport media and rapid tests.
Listen for
Reassuring: claims rising year by year on tube sales. Concerning: claims falling, or driven by COVID-era kits.
Follow-up
Are the rapid tests and RNA extraction kit approved under the scheme still sold?

Q51If time permitsBlood and diagnostics

Which laboratory chains, hospitals and government buyers take your blood-collection tubes, and how concentrated is the business?

Why it matters
Chains are consolidating laboratory testing, which concentrates buying power (Section A4.5).
Our context
The MD named Thyrocare, Metropolis, Dr. Lal PathLabs, SRL and Pathkind as customers in May-2021 and added Vijay Diagnostics in Feb-2022.
Listen for
Reassuring: multi-year contracts with the large chains. Concerning: sales to a few chains on short tenders.
Follow-up
Do you export tubes, and which IVD registrations (EU IVDR, Saudi SFDA) do you hold or plan?

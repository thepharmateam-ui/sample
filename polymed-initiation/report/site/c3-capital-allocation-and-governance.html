Capital allocation and governance

Q134Must-askCapital allocation

Rs. 4.8bn of QIP money was still unused at 31-Mar-2026, and only Rs. 620mn had gone to plant capex against Rs. 3.0bn scheduled. When will it be deployed, and why was part of the idle money placed in hybrid funds and a PMS?

Why it matters
The monitoring agency flagged the placement as not in line with the placement document (Section A9).
Our context
The hybrid-fund placement was Rs. 719mn, and Rs. 50mn sat in a PMS.
Listen for
Reassuring: a deployment schedule and a revised treasury policy. Concerning: capex slipping further.
Follow-up
Who approves treasury investments?

Q135Must-askGovernance

What does Vitromed Healthcare make for Polymed, how is the price set, what margin does it earn, and is there a plan to bring the work in-house or merge it?

Why it matters
Rs. 870mn of job work went to a promoter partnership in FY26 (Section A9).
Our context
That was 84% of all job-work charges and 5.2% of standalone revenue (our calculation).
Listen for
Reassuring: benchmarked pricing and a timeline to absorb the capacity. Concerning: no plan and no benchmark.
Follow-up
Does the audit committee see Vitromed's cost structure?

Q136Must-askGovernance

Who owns and controls Ezekiel Global Business Solutions and Zetta Matrix Consulting, and why were they reclassified into the promoter group?

Why it matters
The reclassifications lifted reported promoter holding and narrowed the effective float (Section A9).
Our context
They held 14.0% and 9.4% in FY20 as public shareholders; the promoter group now holds 62.4%.
Listen for
Reassuring: a clear explanation matching the regulatory definition of promoter group. Concerning: evasion.
Follow-up
Do any other significant shareholders have links to the promoter family?

Q137If time permitsGovernance

What land and liabilities come with Himalayan Mineral Water, and what is the exposure in the NCLAT appeal?

Why it matters
An unusual acquisition route (insolvency) with legacy liabilities.
Our context
Polymed paid Rs. 332mn under the resolution plan to secure land next to the Haridwar site.
Listen for
Reassuring: ring-fenced liabilities. Concerning: open-ended claims.
Follow-up
When will the land be used?

Q138If time permitsCapital allocation

What are your criteria for the next acquisition, and how are integration and post-deal performance governed?

Why it matters
Two deals are not yet profitable, and management said it wants to pause and absorb.
Our context
Contingent consideration payable was Rs. 352mn at 31-Mar-2026.
Listen for
Reassuring: return hurdles and board-level post-deal reviews. Concerning: deal flow driven by availability.
Follow-up
What return on capital do you expect from PendraCare and Citieffe by FY30?

Q139If time permitsCapital allocation

What would lead the board to raise the dividend payout from its current level?

Why it matters
Payout is low relative to cash and returns; capital allocation is the swing factor for returns (Section A9).
Our context
The FY26 dividend was Rs. 3.5 a share, a payout of 11% (our calculation); net cash was ~Rs. 5.0bn.
Listen for
Reassuring: a stated policy linked to capex and M&A needs. Concerning: no framework.
Follow-up
Would you consider a buyback?

Q140If time permitsGovernance

How is executive pay linked to performance beyond the profit-linked commission, and how does the board benchmark it?

Why it matters
The MD's pay ratio is high (Section A9).
Our context
The MD earned 497x the median employee in FY26; promoter-family pay was 14.7% of standalone PAT (our calculation).
Listen for
Reassuring: long-term targets tied to returns. Concerning: none beyond the commission.
Follow-up
Are the professional CEOs on long-term incentive plans?

Q141If time permitsGovernance

What is the status of the CGST show-cause notice listed at 31-Mar-2024, and of the stamp-duty order on past share allotments?

Why it matters
Contingent items that dropped out of, or were added to, the disclosures (Section A9).
Our context
The CGST notice was Rs. 424mn; the stamp-duty demand was Rs. 25mn, stayed by the Delhi High Court.
Listen for
Reassuring: resolved or well-defended. Concerning: unclear status.
Follow-up
Are there any other disputes above the materiality threshold?

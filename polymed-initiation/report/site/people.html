Board and key management

At 31 Mar 2026 Polymed's board had 12 directors: 4 executive (our calculation) and 8 non-executive, of whom 5 were independent. The Aug-2024 placement document had shown 12 directors, 7 of them independent. The changes since then:

  • Independent terms ended. Dr Shailendra Raj Mehta and Dr Sandeep Bhargava left on completing their terms on 26 Sep 2024.
  • The chairman was reclassified. D.R. Mehta's tenure as an independent director ended at the 29th AGM in Sep 2024, and shareholders re-appointed him as a non-executive, non-independent director. The 31st AGM re-appointed him by special resolution, which is required because he is over 75 years old; 4.0% of institutional votes were cast against.
  • One independent joined. Vishal Gupta joined on 28 Oct 2024.
  • Executive seats were added. Mukulika Baid, a promoter-family non-executive director, stepped down on 6 May 2025, the day Vishal Baid became an executive director; Pankaj Kumar Gupta, the head of operations, followed on 8 Aug 2025.

The executive team below the board combines long-serving Polymed managers with hires made in 2024-26 for strategy, India and APAC, Brazil and renal, and the chief executives of the acquired companies.

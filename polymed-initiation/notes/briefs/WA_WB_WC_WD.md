## WA: infusion, vascular access and oncology, anaesthesia/respiratory/critical care, components/OEM

**`draft/20_A4_1_infusion.md` – A4.1 Infusion therapy** (the largest and most important chapter; ~1,800 words, 4-5 exhibits). Cover:
- **Portfolio:** IV cannulas, conventional and safety. Brands include Polyshield, Polyguard, Polysafety, Polywin, Polycan, Polyflon and Neonovo. Also infusion sets and burettes, stopcocks, extension lines, needle-free connectors, and closed-system or safety sets.
- **Unit of demand and demand drivers:** cannulas per inpatient stay and re-sites, patient-days, procedure volumes, and safety conversion driven by sharps mandates.
- **Position:** #3 global IV cannula maker with ~11% share and ~600mn pieces; the plants that make cannulas; capacity.
- **Revenue:** history FY20-FY26 and 1QFY27; the share of revenue over time.
- **Pricing:** Rs. 10-20 price points; export vs India pricing if disclosed.
- **Customers and channel:** hospitals, distributors, own brand vs OEM, GPO contracts in the US.
- **US:** the FDA 510(k) list (all infusion products), US revenue and the tariff history.
- **Europe:** the destocking cycle; MDR.
- **Competition:** BD, B. Braun, ICU Medical/Smiths, Terumo, Vygon, plus Chinese makers (Weigao, KDL and others) and Indian makers.
- **Growth drivers and margin drivers:** automation, needle-making, sterilization, mix toward safety products.
- **What to watch.**

**`draft/21_A4_2_vascular_oncology.md` – A4.2 Vascular access and oncology (Plan1Health).**

**`draft/25_A4_6_anaesthesia_resp_critical.md` – A4.6 Anaesthesia, respiratory and critical care.**

**`draft/29_A4_10_components_oem.md` – A4.10 Components, OEM and contract manufacturing.**

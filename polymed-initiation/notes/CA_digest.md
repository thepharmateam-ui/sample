## A1 – Company overview, history and strategy (PolyMed 1.0/2.0/3.0, Vision 2030)

- HB frames history in three phases: 1997 IPO and 25 bootstrapped years building infusion leadership; 2021 – "PolyMed started 2.0 journey, which we call the inflection point in our history of the company" (first institutional fundraise, multifold capacity, entry into cardiology, critical care, orthopaedics, first acquisitions); FY27 onward "PolyMed 3.0, a phase we are calling Ascent" [TR1Q27S p.2]
- *(also A3)* Vision 2030 goal: "double our revenue by FY30" via organic + inorganic expansion, "leveraging technology to scale high complexity verticals globally and deepening direct customer access, especially in international markets"; HB calls it an "audacious goal" and aims to make Polymed "a global medtech MNC out of India" [TR1Q27S p.2]
- *(also A3)* Mix of the doubling: HB – "current avatar company does around 80% organic business and 20% inorganic business ... we'll probably continue at a similar run rate" [TR1Q27S p.7]; RG – standalone Polymed "will grow faster than the underlying subsidiaries" [TR1Q27S p.12]
- *(also A3)* FY25 delivered vs guidance: standalone revenue Rs1,307cr -> Rs1,601cr (~22.5%) vs 22–24% guided – "we were very accurately able to forecast our revenue"; EBITDA margin +>100 bps vs 100–250 bps guided; consolidated ~Rs1,670cr (+~21.5%), EBITDA Rs451cr (27.1%), PAT Rs338.6cr [TR4Q25 p.3]
- *(also A3)* Peak-momentum quarter: consolidated Q3 revenue +~24.9% to Rs424cr; 9M +23.2% to Rs1,229cr vs 22–24% FY25 guide; 9M EBITDA margin 27.4% (+~95 bps vs "100 to 150 bps" guided) [CA note: recalled as "100-250 bps" on the 7-May-2025 call, TR4Q25 p.3]; ROCE ~23.9% excluding undeployed QIP [TR3Q25 p.3-4]
- *(also A3)* Growth framing (to Bharat Shah, ASK): 2010–2020 CAGR "14% to 15% ... the Company was capital starved"; last 3 years ~20%+; "the new normal for this Company should be 20% plus growth ... the orbit has changed from 14 to 20"; exports "north of 20%" targeted; margins to rise "50-100 bps easily" a year for 4–5 years; risk – "only things can go wrong when we make bad quality product" [TR3Q25 p.13-14]

## A2 – Portfolio mix and business-model shift

- HB declined to disclose oncology/cardiology revenues: "we don't specifically call out those revenues ... confidentially, we don't want to share" [TR4Q26 p.17]
- Price points: infusion devices "INR10, INR15, INR20"; orthopaedic/cardiology products "thousands of rupees or sometimes north of INR20,000" [TR4Q26 p.18]
- *(also A6)* Strategic focus areas for next 5 years: orthopaedics, cardiology, neonatology, oncology, renal care – "helps us to even get a better gross margin" [TR4Q26 p.10]
- *(also A3)* HB: "We are currently transitioning from low technology products to high complexity, high-growth segment. This isn't just a change in our product list, it's a fundamental upgrade of our business model"; core infusion "is generating significant profits, enabling us to invest into these new high-growth areas"; aim "one of its kind medtech platform ... creating a larger entry barriers" [TR3Q26 p.3]
- *(also A6)* Portfolio breadth: ~250 products of ~50,000 in medtech; 50–80 products in development for FY26–29 launch; not yet in urology, gastroenterology, respiratory care, cardiac surgery, peripheral vascular, neurology; orthopaedics roadmap trauma → plates → joints (knee, hip), spine [TR2Q26 p.12]
- *(also A4.1)* 9M mix: infusion ~70% of revenue (Rs800cr of Rs1,181cr, growth ~25%), renal ~8–9% (Rs104cr, +~56% vs ">50%" projected), other 5–6 categories Rs277cr each "under 5%" – not disclosed separately [TR3Q25 p.5, p.17]
- *(also A3)* Volumes: devices sold 32 crore units in Q3 (26 crore a year earlier), 96 crore in 9M (+19%) vs revenue +23% – HB cites this as evidence of pricing; average realisation ~Rs12–13 per device (analyst) because "we are mostly a consumable Company. Consumables are always priced at low point" [TR3Q25 p.4, p.10]

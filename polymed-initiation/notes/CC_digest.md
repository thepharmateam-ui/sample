## A3 – Guidance record (FY21–FY25) versus delivery

The pattern is consistent. Total revenue guidance was met or beaten every year: FY22 17-18% was met, and FY23 "20%+" and FY24 22-24% (raised from 20%+) were exceeded. The mix inside the total missed persistently. Domestic growth and renal fell short three years running while exports beat. Dialysis-machine, US and PLI targets slipped repeatedly. Capex guidance rose each time it was restated. Management says openly that it "starts with a moderate number" and revises after 1Q/2Q. [TR3Q24b p.7]

## A2 – Business model, channel, pricing and costs
- Channel model:
  - India runs through its own reps plus distributors: "close to around 275 people on the ground" (Nov-2021).
  - Overseas sales go through "large distribution partners. We do not have any people on the ground" (Nov-2021).
  - By May-2024 Europe was still "mainly through distributors" apart from Italy and some resident sales managers. The model is to train the distributors' sales forces.
  - The US is also served via distributors and GPOs.
  [TR2Q22 p.14] [TR4Q24 p.16] [TR4Q23 p.14, p.16]
- India channel changes (Feb-2022): the company moved from a single distributor per state to multiple distributors, some aligned to a single hospital chain. Dialysis has one distributor per chain; diagnostics and blood banking have separate specialist distributors. A single corporate-hospital price list with volume discounts started from 1-Apr-2022. Payment cycles run 3-4 months for good hospitals and 6-9 months for bad ones. [TR3Q22 p.10-11]
- India reach: 5000+ hospitals and 250+ associates (Oct-2019) → 5500+ and 275+ (2020) → 6500+ and 275+ (May-2022) → 8,000+ hospitals and 350+ associates (Nov-2023) → 400+ associates (2024 decks). The MD's own count is ~5,000 hospitals reached directly; he tracks 300-400 top hospitals and says each rep covers 15-20 hospitals. [DK2019 p.4] [DKJUN20 p.4] [DKMAY22 p.8] [DK2Q24 p.4] [DKJUL24 p.3] [TR3Q23 p.21] [TR3Q24b p.9, p.13]
- Field-force counts do not reconcile:
  - ~250 "feet on the ground" (Feb-2022; CC-0185) against a 275 → 375 plan (May-2022; CC-0250/0251).

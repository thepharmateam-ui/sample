# Symbiotec's Vegan D3 Bet Enters a Crowded Niche

Global vitamin D3 is a **~185-tonne molecule** that the industry reports as a ~14,740-tonne business (calculation: 14,740 t of 500,000 IU/g powder ÷ 80 = ~184 t of pure 40 MIU/g D3; capacity from [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); unit definitions from [Garden Bio H1 2026 report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-15/1225473446.PDF)). Chinese capacity tables count 500,000 IU/g feed powder, China holds **83%** of that capacity, and actual demand of roughly 6,750–10,800 t of powder (about 85–135 t of pure D3) leaves the sector running at roughly half to three-quarters of its capacity ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf); calculation: 6,750 t ÷ 80 = 84 t and 10,836 t ÷ 80 = 135 t of pure D3; utilisation 6,750 ÷ 14,600 = 46% and 10,836 ÷ 14,740 = 74%). Zhejiang Garden Bio leads with ~28% of capacity, a self-claimed 30–40% share and ~70% of the NF-grade lanolin cholesterol that every conventional producer needs ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF); calculation: 4,140 t ÷ 14,740 t = 28.1%). DSM-Firmenich has reportedly cut back its own D3 production, and its animal nutrition and vitamins business is being sold to CVC ([Dongwu Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202507161710004178_1.pdf); [dsm-firmenich, Feb 2026](https://our-company.dsm-firmenich.com/en/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/dsm-firmenich-announces-agreement-to-divest-animal-nutrition-and-health-to-cvc-capital-partners.html)). That leaves Fermenta as the only large integrated D3 maker outside China (analyst inference, based on [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf)). Feed takes 85–90% of the tonnes, but feed prices swing roughly 5–10x while human-nutrition and pharma grades hold steady ([Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q); [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf); [Hzeyun](https://m.hzeyun.com/research/2596346.html); calculation: from the 2024 trough of RMB 56.5/kg to RMB 255/kg traded = 4.5x, and to RMB 400–600/kg ex-works quotes = 7–11x). Feed D3 is now trading at **~RMB 95–105/kg (October 2026)**, against RMB 600+ list quotes in 2025 ([Mysteel](https://list1.m.mysteel.com/zhishi/slwssD3jg.html); [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/888396671_135357); [Sina/Securities Times](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-06-28/doc-infcsauc1704880.shtml)). Nutrition and pharma therefore earn more than half of a producer-level market worth only a few hundred million dollars (calculation, detailed under "Volume and value split": ~USD 130–140m human-grade against ~USD 100m feed at October 2026 prices; inputs from [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf); [Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q); [Mysteel](https://list1.m.mysteel.com/zhishi/slwssD3jg.html)). Non-animal D3 exists but is small (analyst inference, based on [Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lup/publication/f49ca442-2470-4d4e-95d1-1af92d805d5c); [Food Chemistry](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0308814620304891); [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946)). Lichen and microalgae hold one or two micrograms of D3 per gram of biomass, and no commercial launch of yeast-fermented D3 was found ([Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lup/publication/f49ca442-2470-4d4e-95d1-1af92d805d5c); [Food Chemistry](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0308814620304891); not found in sources searched to Oct 2026, with academic titres in [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39480248)). Semi-synthesis from plant sterols (phytosterols) is the only route that scales (analyst inference, based on [Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lup/publication/f49ca442-2470-4d4e-95d1-1af92d805d5c); [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946); [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/biotech/fermenta-biotech-to-invest-rs-110-crore-for-dahej-plant-expansion-18512)). It is already commercial at Kerui/Chundi in China and at Fermenta in India, which won FSSAI approval in July 2026 and targets a ~1 t/yr line for FY28 ([Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF); [vegconomist](https://vegconomist.com/ingredients/fermenta-biotech-receives-indian-approval-plant-based-vitamin-d3-ingredient/); [Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf); [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/biotech/fermenta-biotech-to-invest-rs-110-crore-for-dahej-plant-expansion-18512); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/fermenta-biotech-expands-plant-based-vitamin-d3-capacity-dahej-126072800596_1.html)). Symbiotec plans a 40 MIU/g Ph.Eur.-grade vegan D3 built on its in-house phytosterol fermentation and photochemistry, with dedicated capacity from FY28 ([nutra.symbiotec.com](https://nutra.symbiotec.com/); [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf)). That fits its platform better than it would fit any other Indian steroid maker's (analyst inference, based on [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf), which describes Symbiotec as one of two Indian fermentation steroid API makers with regulated-market approvals). But Symbiotec arrives third, holds no vitamin D drug master file (DMF), European certificate of suitability (CEP) or FSSAI approval, and management's **US$600m** estimate of the niche is larger than the entire producer-level D3 market ([FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs); [EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72); [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf); no Symbiotec FSSAI vitamin D approval found in sources searched; the entry order and the market comparison are analyst inference, set out in the competitor and economics sections below). Analysts should value vegan D3 at zero in FY27–29 base estimates (analyst inference, based on [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf); [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/); [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946)). A one-tonne line is worth ₹7–88 cr of revenue depending almost entirely on a vegan price premium that no supplier publishes (calculation: 1,000 kg × USD 800–10,000/kg × ₹88/USD, an FX assumption, = ₹7–88 cr; USD 800–1,500/kg pharma crystal from [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/); no published vegan D3 bulk price found). Plant cholesterol and calcifediol are more valuable adjacent products (analyst inference, based on [Evonik](https://www.evonik.com/en/products/hc/pr_52050742.html); [EDQM calcifediol](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/1295); [Investing.com](https://ng.investing.com/news/company-news/faes-farma-fy-2025-slides-23-revenue-surge-masks-profit-pressures-93CH-2367801); [OPKO 10-K](https://www.opko.com/investors/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-076596/opk-20251231.htm)).

## China's lanolin chain controls 83% of a 185-tonne molecule

### Capacity is counted in feed powder, not in D3

Every capacity number in this market has to be converted before it can be compared with anything Symbiotec or Fermenta might build (analyst inference, based on [Garden Bio H1 2026 report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-15/1225473446.PDF); [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). Garden Bio's filings define pure crystalline D3 as 40 MIU/g and feed-grade powder as 500,000 IU/g, so **80 t of feed powder contains 1 t of cholecalciferol** ([Garden Bio H1 2026 report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-15/1225473446.PDF); calculation: 40,000,000 IU/g ÷ 500,000 IU/g = 80). Chinese broker and data-compiler tables are the main producer-level capacity data available, and they use this 500 KIU/g basis ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf); [Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf)). Tianfeng's December 2024 table puts global nameplate capacity at **~14,740 t (≈184 t pure D3)**, with China at 83.04% ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); calculation: 14,740 t ÷ 80 = 184 t). BP Equities, citing Boyar Hexun, put it at 14,600 t around 2019, with China at 13,100 t ([BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf)). Other totals include 10,840 t (Pacific Securities, which lists Hisound at 800 t and Fermenta at 300 t) and 12,800 t (Hzeyun) ([Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf); [Hzeyun](https://m.hzeyun.com/research/2596346.html)). The spread comes mostly from out-of-date figures for Hisound, DSM, Fermenta and Zhejiang Medicine (analyst inference, based on [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf), which flags the DSM, Fermenta and Zhejiang Medicine figures as dated; [Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf)).

Dishman Carbogen Amcis's Veenendaal site in the Netherlands (formerly Solvay's) makes cholesterol, vitamin D2 and vitamin D analogues rather than bulk D3, and its cholecalciferol CEP was withdrawn in 2023 ([Carbogen Amcis brochure](https://www.carbogen-amcis.com/images/brochures/cgam/2023/Cholesterol_Vitamin_D_Analogs_CARBOGEN-AMCIS.pdf); [MAI CDMO profile](https://mai-cdmo.com/carbogen-amcis-veenendaal); [EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72)). ### Overcapacity and concentration let producers, not demand, set the price

In 2019 China produced **5,650 t against 13,100 t of capacity**, about 43% utilisation, and exported 4,050 t of it ([BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf); calculation: 5,650 ÷ 13,100 = 43%). Tianfeng's high theoretical demand estimate of 10,836 t assumes 8.4 g of D3 per tonne across 1.29 bn t of global feed ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). Even on that basis, nameplate capacity exceeds need by ~35% (calculation: 14,740 t ÷ 10,836 t = 1.36; inputs from [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). Price therefore reflects how disciplined producers are, not physical scarcity (analyst inference, based on [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf); [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Sina, Mar 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-03-24/doc-inequprn0560695.shtml)). When China's market regulator (SAMR) conditionally cleared the Garden–DSM 7-DHC joint venture in October 2019, it measured market concentration (HHI) at **2,236 for veterinary D3 and 2,798 for human D3 globally** ([SAMR decision](https://www.shanwei.gov.cn/shanwei/swzdly/jgsf/jgsf03/content/post_452053.html)). The remedies required Garden to sell cholesterol to rival D3 makers on fair, reasonable and non-discriminatory (FRAND) terms and not to unreasonably restrict output, and they lapsed automatically after five years ([SAMR decision](https://www.shanwei.gov.cn/shanwei/swzdly/jgsf/jgsf03/content/post_452053.html)). The lapse, around October 2024, fell within Chinese producers' "stop quoting, stop signing" period, which began in May 2024 and preceded the 2024–25 price spike (analyst inference on timing: 16 Oct 2019 decision plus five years; based on [SAMR decision](https://www.shanwei.gov.cn/shanwei/swzdly/jgsf/jgsf03/content/post_452053.html); [Sina, Mar 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/roll/2025-03-24/doc-inequprn0560695.shtml)). The industry has history here: the European Commission's 2001 vitamins cartel decision fined Solvay €9.1m and BASF €7.56m over the D3 market ([EU Decision 2003/2/EC](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2003/2%281%29/oj/eng/pdf)).

Two capacity details matter for Symbiotec in particular (analyst inference, based on [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). Hisound is No. 2 in Tianfeng's table and led prices up, raising its quote to RMB 600/kg in April 2025 ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/888396671_135357)). One source describes it as a controlled subsidiary of Zhejiang Xianju, a Chinese steroid API maker that Symbiotec's RHP names as a competitor, but Xianju's 2024 annual report does not list Hisound among its subsidiaries ([Tencent](https://gu.qq.com/sz002332/gp/notice/nos1220987252); [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); [Xianju 2024 AR](https://www.xjpharma.com/Upload/202504/20250429131008_4153.pdf)). If the link is real, a direct steroid competitor already runs a fifth of global D3 capacity (analyst inference, based on [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); calculation: 3,000 t ÷ 14,740 t = 20.4%). Separately, Gansu Jiahui's new D3 project faces strict environmental review, and dsm-firmenich says Chinese expansion plans were pushed back to mid-2026 and still described them as delayed in June 2026 ([Gelonghui](https://m.gelonghui.com/p/2089901); [dsm-firmenich Q1 2026 outlook](https://www.dsm-firmenich.com/anh/en_NA/news/articles/vitamin-market-update-q1-2026.html); [dsm-firmenich Q3 2026 outlook](https://www.dsm-firmenich.com/anh/en_NA/news/articles/vitamin-market-update-q3-2026.html)).

### DSM's retreat leaves Fermenta as the only large non-Chinese maker

A Chinese broker, Dongwu Securities, reports that DSM gradually shut its vitamin C, vitamin B and D3 capacity during 2023–24 ([Dongwu Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202507161710004178_1.pdf)). Garden says it **renewed its D3 contract with DSM in April 2026**; separately, it holds 10–15-year agreements with DSM for cholesterol, 7-DHC and 25-OH-D3 ([Garden Bio IR, Apr 2026](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225257262.PDF); [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF); [Sina, Jul 2024](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/relnews/cn/2024-07-06/doc-inccemmq1357995.shtml)). No DSM statement confirms a closure (not found in sources searched to Oct 2026; the [dsm-firmenich vitamin D page](https://www.dsm-firmenich.com/en/businesses/health-nutrition-care/products/vitamins/vitamin-d.html) still names Sisseln and the [dsm-firmenich Q1 2026 outlook](https://www.dsm-firmenich.com/anh/en_NA/news/articles/vitamin-market-update-q1-2026.html) does not mention an exit). DSM still holds valid EDQM certificates for cholecalciferol, one issued for "Site Sisseln" in July 2025 and another revised as recently as February 2026 ([EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72)). That fits DSM remaining a regulated supplier of pharma-grade forms made partly from Chinese material (analyst inference, based on [EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72); [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2026](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225257262.PDF); [Dongwu Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202507161710004178_1.pdf)). Its Animal Nutrition & Health unit, which includes vitamins, is being sold to CVC at an enterprise value of ~€2.2bn and split into an "Essential Products Company" (vitamins) and a premix company ([dsm-firmenich, Feb 2026](https://our-company.dsm-firmenich.com/en/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/dsm-firmenich-announces-agreement-to-divest-animal-nutrition-and-health-to-cvc-capital-partners.html)). dsm-firmenich keeps 20% and a long-term vitamin supply agreement for its human and pet businesses, and closing is expected at end-2026 ([dsm-firmenich, Feb 2026](https://our-company.dsm-firmenich.com/en/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/dsm-firmenich-announces-agreement-to-divest-animal-nutrition-and-health-to-cvc-capital-partners.html)). That leaves Fermenta as the only large, integrated source outside China (analyst inference, based on [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Dongwu Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202507161710004178_1.pdf); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf)). It describes itself as "one of the world's three largest manufacturers of pharmaceutical-grade Vitamin D3", makes its own cholesterol from wool grease at Dahej, and in 2021 claimed ~25% of the human-grade D3 API market ([Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf); [CARE, Oct 2025](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202510131020_Fermenta_Biotech_Limited.pdf)). Even Fermenta has its feed-grade D3 500 made by a toll manufacturer in Germany rather than in its own plants ([Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf)). That suggests feed-grade D3 is not economic to make outside China (analyst inference, based on [Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf)).

## Non-animal D3 scales only through phytosterol chemistry

"Vegan" D3 covers four chemically different supply chains that marketing copy tends to blur: lichen extracts, microalgae, yeast fermentation of sterol precursors, and semi-synthesis from plant sterols (analyst inference, based on [vegconomist](https://vegconomist.com/ingredients/fermenta-biotech-receives-indian-approval-plant-based-vitamin-d3-ingredient/) and [Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf), which describe the same product as lichen-based and phytosterol-derived respectively; [Vitamin Retailer](https://vitaminretailer.com/synthetic-vs-vegan-d3-impact-on-health-sustainability/)). Only the last has shown tonne-scale capacity (analyst inference, based on [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946); [SINOQUAL](https://www.halalkosher.com/zh-CN/articles/legacy-39); [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/biotech/fermenta-biotech-to-invest-rs-110-crore-for-dahej-plant-expansion-18512)).

Lichen is the brand-led segment (analyst inference, based on [NutraIngredients, 2012](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2012/12/07/Lichen-based-vegan-vitamin-D3-gains-momentum-as-Nordic-Naturals-introduces-new-product/); [Nutritional Outlook](https://www.nutritionaloutlook.com/view/vegadelight-earns-euromonitor-recognition-top-selling-vegan-vitamin-d3-ingredient-us)). ESB Developments' Vitashine (UK) has sold wild-harvested lichen D3 since about 2012 ([NutraIngredients, 2012](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2012/12/07/Lichen-based-vegan-vitamin-D3-gains-momentum-as-Nordic-Naturals-introduces-new-product/)). Euromonitor certified Nutraland's Vegadelight as the top-selling vegan D3 ingredient in the US by 2025 business-to-business value, though Nutraland commissioned the research and no volumes were disclosed ([Nutritional Outlook](https://www.nutritionaloutlook.com/view/vegadelight-earns-euromonitor-recognition-top-selling-vegan-vitamin-d3-ingredient-us)). A long tail of Chinese and Indian sellers lists 100,000 IU/g lichen powders ([Richtek](https://www.richtekltd.com/VitaminD3fromlichen); [Herbuno](https://herbuno.com/products/vitamin-d3-100-000-iu-g-from-lichen-extract)). None discloses capacity, and the biology explains why (capacity not found in sources searched to Oct 2026; the explanation is analyst inference, based on [Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lup/publication/f49ca442-2470-4d4e-95d1-1af92d805d5c)). Lund University measured just **0.67–2.04 µg of D3 per gram** of dry *Cladina arbuscula*, with its precursor 7-DHC below detection ([Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lup/publication/f49ca442-2470-4d4e-95d1-1af92d805d5c)). At those levels, a kilogram of pure D3 needs roughly 490–1,490 t of dry lichen (calculation: 10^9 µg ÷ 2.04 µg/g = 490 t and 10^9 µg ÷ 0.67 µg/g = 1,490 t, assuming 100% recovery; D3 content from [Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lup/publication/f49ca442-2470-4d4e-95d1-1af92d805d5c)). Direct extraction therefore yields kilograms of D3 for premium oils, not tonnes, and any tonne-scale "lichen" claim implies an extra conversion step (analyst inference, based on [Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lup/publication/f49ca442-2470-4d4e-95d1-1af92d805d5c)). Sourcing is opaque enough that a Vitamin Retailer commentary called undisclosed lichen species "problematic", and Innobio sells stable-isotope proof of plant origin as a selling point ([Vitamin Retailer](https://vitaminretailer.com/synthetic-vs-vegan-d3-impact-on-health-sustainability/); [NutraIngredients, isotope-verified D3](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Product-innovations/isotope-verified-vegan-d3-from-pine)).

Microalgae is thinner still (analyst inference, based on [Food Chemistry](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0308814620304891); [DTU](https://www.food.dtu.dk/english/newsarchive/2025/07/researchers-aim-to-upscale-new-source-of-vitamin-d3-and-omega-3-fatty-acids)). AIDP's VegD3, supplied as pure crystal since 2016 under self-affirmed GRAS (generally recognised as safe) status, is the only named business-to-business algae D3 ingredient found ([NutraIngredients, 2019](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2019/04/29/AIDP-There-is-a-significant-market-potential-for-vegan-D3/)). *Nannochloropsis oceanica* produces at most ~1 µg/g after UV-B exposure ([Food Chemistry](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0308814620304891)), and DTU's AlgaeVita scale-up project was still at the R&D stage in July 2025 ([DTU](https://www.food.dtu.dk/english/newsarchive/2025/07/researchers-aim-to-upscale-new-source-of-vitamin-d3-and-omega-3-fatty-acids)). Algae D3 makes sense as a by-product of omega-3 algae cultivation, not as a supply route in its own right (analyst inference, based on [DTU](https://www.food.dtu.dk/english/newsarchive/2025/07/researchers-aim-to-upscale-new-source-of-vitamin-d3-and-omega-3-fatty-acids); [Food Chemistry](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0308814620304891)).

Fermentation works technically, but no commercial D3 product was found (analyst inference, based on [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39480248); [EFSA Journal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159549/)). Engineered *S. cerevisiae* yeast reached **3.80 g/L of 7-DHC in 24 hours in a 50 L fermenter** at Jiangnan University, and 4.28 g/L in a 10 L vessel in another study ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39480248); [Metabolic Engineering](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1096717624000843)). At ~4 g/L, one 100 m³ batch would yield ~400 kg of 7-DHC, which is large relative to vegan demand (calculation: 4 g/L × 100,000 L = 400 kg; titre from [Metabolic Engineering](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1096717624000843); the comparison with vegan demand is analyst inference). Yet no commercial launch of yeast-made 7-DHC converted to D3 was found (not found in sources searched to Oct 2026; see [EFSA Journal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159549/) for the one commercial yeast route, which makes 25-OH-D3). The one commercial yeast route is dsm-firmenich's Hy-D ([EFSA Journal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159549/)). It ferments a sterol (5,7,24-cholestatrienol) with genetically modified *S. cerevisiae* strain CBS 146008 and converts it chemically to **25-OH-D3** for animal feed ([EFSA Journal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159549/)). It is authorised in the EU under Regulation 2024/1070, and EFSA extended its opinion to all animal species in 2025 ([EFSA Journal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159549/); [EUR-Lex 2024/1070](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202401070)). In China, Ruipu Bio is building a "calcifediol cell factory" with the Chinese Academy of Sciences' Tianjin institute ([Pharnexcloud](https://www.pharnexcloud.com/zixun/sx_47012)). The barrier is cost (analyst inference, based on [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF)). Yeast-made 7-DHC has to compete with lanolin cholesterol when feed D3 was quoted at about USD 0.065 per MIU in April 2025 (calculation: RMB 230–250/kg ÷ 500 MIU/kg = RMB 0.46–0.50/MIU ÷ RMB 7.2/USD, an FX assumption, = USD 0.064–0.069/MIU; quote from [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF)). GMO status also sits awkwardly with "natural vegan" marketing (analyst inference, based on [EFSA Journal](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12159549/), which describes the genetically modified production strain).

Semi-synthesis from phytosterols is where the industrial capacity is, and it is Symbiotec's home ground (analyst inference, based on [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946); [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). Kerui Bio (科瑞生物), controlled by Zhejiang Ang Likang since 2023, makes plant-source cholesterol from phytosterols by "**biological fermentation plus multi-step chemical synthesis**" ([Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF); [CLS, 2023](https://www.cls.cn/detail/1328741)). It claims to be China's first mass producer of plant cholesterol and plant vitamin D, and sells mainly to overseas pharma, nutrition and feed companies ([Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF)). Kerui reports **30 t/yr of plant cholesterol** ([PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946)). Its subsidiary Jiangxi Chundi earned **RMB 71.6m of revenue and RMB 15.6m of net profit in 2023**, and has a five-year 25-OH-D3 supply contract with a European company ([PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946)). A certification body's page credits Chundi with lines for 10 t/yr of D3 crystal, 10 t/yr of 25-OH-D3 crystal and 2 t/yr of D2 crystal ([SINOQUAL](https://www.halalkosher.com/zh-CN/articles/legacy-39)). But 30 t of plant cholesterol cannot feed 20 t of crystal at typical multi-step yields, so those are either nameplate figures or lines that also run on lanolin cholesterol (analyst inference; calculation: 20 t of D3 and 25-OH-D3 crystal ÷ 46.9% best-in-class overall D3 yield ≈ 43 t of cholesterol, above 30 t; yield from [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); capacities from [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946) and [SINOQUAL](https://www.halalkosher.com/zh-CN/articles/legacy-39)). Kerui's specialty-intermediates sales declined in 2025 "due to fluctuating overseas customer demand" ([Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF)).

Fermenta's own documents call its VITADEE Green a phytosterol-derived 40 MIU/g D3, though trade press reports it as lichen-based ([Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf); [vegconomist](https://vegconomist.com/ingredients/fermenta-biotech-receives-indian-approval-plant-based-vitamin-d3-ingredient/)). Its patent EP3906247 (priority July 2019) describes an entirely chemical route ([Google Patents, EP3906247](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3906247A1/en)). Crude phytosterol is converted to cholesterol by tosylation, ozonolysis and a Grignard rebuild of the side chain, then to 7-DHC by allylic bromination, then to D3 by sensitised UV photolysis ([Google Patents, EP3906247](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3906247A1/en)). VITADEE Green won FSSAI approval on 6 July 2026 ([Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf); [Fermenta Q1FY27 press release](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/1786437408-Pressrelease-unauditedfinancialresultsofQ1FY27.pdf)). Fermenta's ₹110 cr Dahej programme includes ~₹60 cr for plant-source D3 and calcifediol, reportedly ~1 t/yr of plant D3 plus 50 kg of derivatives, with commercial output targeted for FY28 ([Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/biotech/fermenta-biotech-to-invest-rs-110-crore-for-dahej-plant-expansion-18512); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/fermenta-biotech-expands-plant-based-vitamin-d3-capacity-dahej-126072800596_1.html)). Innobio launched its LocVita D3 in March 2024 from loblolly-pine oil ([NutraIngredients, 2024](https://nutraingredients.com/Article/2024/03/22/innobio-launches-vegan-vitamin-d3-from-pine-oil)). Pine oil is a phytosterol source (analyst inference; Innobio does not disclose its process, per [NutraIngredients, 2024](https://nutraingredients.com/Article/2024/03/22/innobio-launches-vegan-vitamin-d3-from-pine-oil)). It won FSSAI vegan certification in March 2025 but is not sold in mainland China, because no regulation there covers plant-derived D3 ([NutraIngredients, 2025](https://www.nutraingredients.com/Article/2025/07/22/pine-derived-vitamin-d3-approved-as-vegan-ingredient-from-fssai)). Evonik makes plant cholesterol (PhytoChol) at Hanau, but sells it for mRNA lipid nanoparticles rather than D3 ([Evonik](https://www.evonik.com/en/products/hc/pr_52050742.html); [Manufacturing Chemist](https://www.manufacturingchemist.com/evonik-increases-plant-derived-cholesterol-supply-199274)).

## Feed takes 85% of tonnes but under half of today's value

### Volume and value split

No independent body publishes a global split by end use (not found in sources searched to Oct 2026; every split located traces to [Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q), [Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf), [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf) or [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf), cited in the next sentence). Every estimate goes back to Chinese data compilers or to feed tonnage multiplied by an inclusion rate ([Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q); [Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf); [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf); [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). Fhyanbao's 2024 estimate is the most detailed: **7,719 t for feed** (1.287 bn t of feed × 6 g/t), **472 t for food additives** and **943 t for pharma and healthcare**, a total of ~9,134 t that is ~85% feed ([Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q); calculation: 7,719 ÷ 9,134 = 85%). Pacific Securities, citing Boyar Hexun, gives China's domestic split as **90% feed, 6% pharma and 4% food** ([Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf)). An older global data point of 7,250 t of demand, of which 5,500 t was feed, implies ~24% non-feed use once the feed-heavy Chinese mix is diluted ([Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf); calculation: (7,250 − 5,500) ÷ 7,250 = 24%). Converted to pure D3, feed uses roughly 85–100 t a year and all human uses together perhaps 10–25 t (calculation: feed 6,750–7,719 t ÷ 80 = 84–96 t; non-feed share of 10–24% × 6,750–9,134 t of total demand ÷ 80 = 8–27 t, with Fhyanbao's own split giving 1,415 t ÷ 80 = 18 t; inputs from [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf); [Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q); [Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf)).

Value splits very differently (analyst inference, based on [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q)). Published "vitamin D3 market" sizes run from USD 337.5m (Market.us, 2024) to USD 1.7bn (GMI), USD 2.49bn (SkyQuest) and USD 4.2bn (Dataintelo) ([Market.us](https://market.us/report/vitamin-d3-market/); [GMI](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/vitamin-d3-cholecalciferol-market); [SkyQuest](https://www.skyquestt.com/report/vitamin-d3-market); [Dataintelo](https://dataintelo.com/report/global-vitamin-d3-market)). The higher figures can only be counting formulated and finished products, so bottom-up arithmetic is more useful (analyst inference, based on the bottom-up producer values below from [Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q); [Mysteel](https://list1.m.mysteel.com/zhishi/slwssD3jg.html); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf)). About 7,400–7,700 t of feed demand at October 2026's ~RMB 100/kg is worth only about **USD 100m** at the producer level, and about USD 260m at the early-2025 quoted level of ~RMB 250/kg (calculation: 7,416–7,719 t × RMB 100/kg ÷ RMB 7.2/USD = USD 103–107m, and × RMB 250/kg = USD 257–268m; RMB 7.2/USD is an FX assumption; feed tonnage from [ChinaBaogao](https://www.chinabaogao.com/detail/607985.html) and [Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q); prices from [Mysteel](https://list1.m.mysteel.com/zhishi/slwssD3jg.html) and [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF)). Fermenta anchors the human-grade figure ([Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf)). Its FY26 human-nutrition D3 revenue of ₹293 cr (≈USD 33m), combined with its 2021 claim of ~25% of the human D3 API market, implies a human-grade market of roughly **USD 130–140m** (calculation: ₹2,930m ÷ ₹88/USD, an FX assumption, = USD 33.3m; ÷ 25% = USD 133m, or USD 138m at ₹85/USD; inputs from [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf)). That share claim is five years old and comes from the company ([BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf)). The producer-level market is therefore ~USD 230–400m depending on where feed is in its cycle, and human and pharma grades now earn more than half of it (calculation: feed USD 100–270m + human-grade USD 133–138m = USD 233–405m; at October 2026 prices human-grade is 133 ÷ (133 + 103) = 56% of the total; inputs from [ChinaBaogao](https://www.chinabaogao.com/detail/607985.html); [Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q); [Mysteel](https://list1.m.mysteel.com/zhishi/slwssD3jg.html); [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf)). ### Customers buy through formulators, and DSM's sale reopens contracts

Big supplement brands do not disclose their D3 suppliers, and no public source names the supplier for Haleon, Bayer, Nestlé Health Science, Pharmavite, Abbott or By-health (not found in sources searched to Oct 2026; the customer disclosures in [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF) and [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf) name formulators and customer types, not brand owners' D3 sources). What is visible is how the channel works: Garden sells to global formulators and premix makers (DSM, Taiyo, Cargill, Adisseo, BASF and Trouw/Nutreco), with long-term agreements in place with DSM, and **53% of its 2024 export revenue went to the US** ([Sina, Jul 2024](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/relnews/cn/2024-07-06/doc-inccemmq1357995.shtml); [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF)). Fermenta sells crystal, oils and spray-dried powder to **400+ customers in 60+ countries** ([Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf)). Its largest customer is 9% of revenue and its top 10 are 42% ([Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf)). FY26 sales by region were India 39%, Europe 30% and North America 13%, and India rose to 47% in Q1 FY27 ([Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf)). Kingdomway, a D3 maker, also owns the Doctor's Best supplement brand, about 80% of whose sales go through global e-commerce ([10jqka](https://stock.10jqka.com.cn/20260908/c679715272.shtml); [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). The likely pattern is that brand owners buy formulated, pharmacopoeia-grade D3 with DMF or CEP support from DSM, BASF and Fermenta, while premix makers and formulators buy crystal directly from China; this is not confirmed for any named brand (analyst inference, based on [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF); [Sina, Jul 2024](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/relnews/cn/2024-07-06/doc-inccemmq1357995.shtml); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs); [EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72)). The CVC transaction matters here: once it closes at end-2026, dsm-firmenich's human-nutrition arm becomes a long-term contract buyer from the CVC-owned vitamins company ([ThePoultrySite](https://www.thepoultrysite.com/news/2026/02/dsm-firmenich-announces-agreement-to-divest-animal-nutrition-health-to-cvc-capital-partners); [dsm-firmenich, Feb 2026](https://our-company.dsm-firmenich.com/en/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/dsm-firmenich-announces-agreement-to-divest-animal-nutrition-and-health-to-cvc-capital-partners.html)). Human-grade supply relationships are therefore likely to be renegotiated in 2027 (analyst inference, based on [ThePoultrySite](https://www.thepoultrysite.com/news/2026/02/dsm-firmenich-announces-agreement-to-divest-animal-nutrition-health-to-cvc-capital-partners)).

### Pharma D3 is sub-tonne and certificate-gated; analogues are kilogram businesses

India is the standout pharma market (analyst inference, based on [PMC8477736](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8477736/)). In 2020 there were 1,115 vitamin D products in India, of which **457 were cholecalciferol (41%)** ([PMC8477736](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8477736/)). Of those, 63.5% were 60,000 IU strengths sold as capsules, sachets, tablets and injections, up from cholecalciferol being ~10% of preparations around 2013 ([PMC8477736](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8477736/); [IJPS](https://www.ijpsonline.com/articles/vitamin-d-supplements-in-the-indian-market.html)). The latest hard market value is ₹5.38bn for 2018 ([PMC8477736](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8477736/)). Cholecalciferol is under price control by the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA), which pushes formulators toward cheap API, and about one in five Indians is vitamin D deficient ([Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/health/report-warns-india-faces-deepening-vitamin-d-crisis-offers-roadmap-to-address-it); the effect on API sourcing is analyst inference). In tonnes the market is tiny: one kilogram of pure D3 makes ~667,000 doses of 60,000 IU, so even several hundred million sachets a year use well under a tonne (calculation: 1,000 g × 40 MIU/g = 4×10^10 IU ÷ 60,000 IU = 666,667 doses; 500 million sachets ÷ 666,667 = 0.75 t; 40 MIU/g definition from [Garden Bio H1 2026 report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-15/1225473446.PDF); the sachet volume is an illustrative assumption, as no Indian volume data were found). Europe has licensed high-dose products such as Dekristol 20,000 IU and Fultium-D3 20,000 IU ([fachinfo.de](https://fachinfo.de/pdf/011346); [HPRA](https://assets.hpra.ie/products/Human/30446/a6764095-4502-4748-b336-ffb4e3a79e04.pdf)). In the US, the classic prescription vitamin D (Drisdol) is D2, not D3 ([FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs)).

At the regulatory level, pharma-grade D3 is an oligopoly (analyst inference, based on [EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72); [FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs)). Valid EDQM certificates for cholecalciferol are held only by **Fermenta, Garden and DSM-Firmenich** ([EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72)). Dishman Netherlands' was withdrawn in 2023 ([EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72)). Active US DMFs belong to DSM, Fermenta, Dishman Netherlands, Taizhou Hisound, BASF (for a formulated form) and Supreem of Mysore (formulated forms) ([FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs)). NHU and Kingdomway hold none, and Garden's US DMF is inactive ([FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs); [EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72)). Vitamin D analogue APIs are a separate specialist field (analyst inference, based on [FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs)). The repeat filers across calcitriol, alfacalcidol, calcipotriol, calcifediol, paricalcitol and doxercalciferol are Formosa Laboratories, TAPI NL, Cerbios and Carbogen Amcis, plus Chinese entrants (Chongqing Huapont, Nanjing Heron, Anhui Haoyuan, ALP Pharm) ([FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs); [EDQM calcitriol](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/883); [EDQM alfacalcidol](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/1286); [EDQM calcipotriol](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/2011)). The Indian filers are Sun Pharma (doxercalciferol), Neuland and Sai Life (paricalcitol) ([FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs)). The finished drugs are worth tens to low hundreds of millions of dollars per molecule ([Dr. Reddy's 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001135951/000119312514262694/d756631d6k.htm); [Investing.com](https://ng.investing.com/news/company-news/faes-farma-fy-2025-slides-23-revenue-surge-masks-profit-pressures-93CH-2367801); [OPKO 10-K](https://www.opko.com/investors/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-076596/opk-20251231.htm)). US paricalcitol capsules sold ~USD 110m on a moving annual total basis in 2014, Faes Farma's calcifediol franchise sold €63.5m in 2025, and OPKO's Rayaldee earned USD 29.8m in 2025 ([Dr. Reddy's 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001135951/000119312514262694/d756631d6k.htm); [Investing.com](https://ng.investing.com/news/company-news/faes-farma-fy-2025-slides-23-revenue-surge-masks-profit-pressures-93CH-2367801); [OPKO 10-K](https://www.opko.com/investors/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-076596/opk-20251231.htm)). Calcifediol stands out: only **Carbogen Amcis, Formosa and Łukasiewicz** hold valid CEPs, and Garden, Fermenta and Kerui are all moving toward it ([EDQM calcifediol](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/1295); [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946)). Symbiotec holds 44 active US DMFs on the FDA's Q3 2026 list (its RHP cites 43), all for corticosteroids, sex hormones and their intermediates, and **none for any vitamin D compound** ([FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs); [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); calculation: count of Symbiotec entries with active status in the FDA's Q3 2026 DMF spreadsheet).

### Human and pharma grades follow a separate, steadier price ladder

Boyar Hexun describes food and pharma D3 prices as "relatively stable", and Fermenta's results bear this out ([Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf); [Fhyanbao](https://www.fhyanbao.com/article/Y3yu4i1q); [Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf)). In Q1 FY27 its human-nutrition revenue was ₹76.5 cr, up 24% quarter on quarter with volumes up 18%, while feed realisations collapsed ([Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf)). Comparing grades on a pure-D3 basis helps: at RMB 100/kg, feed powder holds 12.5 g of D3 per kg, which works out to **~USD 1,100/kg of pure D3** (calculation: RMB 100/kg × 80 = RMB 8,000/kg of pure D3 ÷ RMB 7.2/USD, an FX assumption, = USD 1,111/kg; potency definitions from [Garden Bio H1 2026 report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-15/1225473446.PDF); price from [Mysteel](https://list1.m.mysteel.com/zhishi/slwssD3jg.html)). Food-grade D3 100 (100,000 IU/g) was quoted at **USD 13.2/kg FOB Shanghai in June 2025**, ≈USD 5,300/kg of pure D3, though formulation cost dominates at that strength ([ChemAnalyst, Jun 2025](https://www.chemanalyst.com/NewsAndDeals/NewsDetails/rising-demand-and-lean-inventories-push-vitamin-d3-prices-higher-in-china-and-us-37514); calculation: USD 13.2/kg × 400 = USD 5,280/kg of pure D3, since 40 MIU/g ÷ 100,000 IU/g = 400; the formulation-cost point is analyst inference). North American feed contracts ran at US$35–42/kg in September 2025, with Canada higher because it has fewer approved suppliers ([dsm-firmenich Q4 2025 outlook](https://www.dsm-firmenich.com/anh/en_NA/news/articles/vitamin-market-outlook-q4-2025.html)). Feed-grade 25-OH-D3 is reported at ~RMB 4,000/kg, an unverified figure ([Gelonghui](https://m.gelonghui.com/p/2089901)). No public bulk price was found for EP/USP crystal, D3 oil or vegan crystal; these appear to be quote-based (not found in sources searched to Oct 2026; for example, [MilliporeSigma](https://merckmillipore.com/CL/en/product/Vitamin-D3-cholecalciferol-cryst.-1-g-40-Mio.I.U.,MDA_CHEM-500936) lists 40 MIU/g crystal as "request pricing").

### Cholesterol, not lanolin, is the cost chokepoint

Conventional D3 is made from NF-grade (≥95%) cholesterol extracted from wool grease ([Jiemian, 2020](https://www.jiemian.com/article/4597880.html); [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). Global NF cholesterol capacity is only **~1,820 t/yr**: Garden 1,200 t, NK (Singapore) 250 t, Dishman 250 t and Nippon Fine Chemical 120 t ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Pacific Securities](https://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202408051639134632_1.pdf)). The non-Garden figures date from 2014, and Garden holds **~70% of global NF lanolin cholesterol**, although Garden's own April 2026 IR record gives a current NF-grade design capacity of 840 t/yr plus 700 t/yr of feed-grade cholesterol ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2026](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225257262.PDF)). China's GB 7300.202-2019 standard allows only lanolin-derived cholesterol for feed D3 ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). Raw wool grease is plentiful; Garden calls supply "ample" ([Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF); [Garden Bio 2024 AR](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-03-25/1222880975.PDF)). The advantage lies in converting it to cholesterol (analyst inference, based on [Garden Bio 2024 AR](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-03-25/1222880975.PDF), where Garden attributes its lower D3 cost to self-made NF cholesterol; [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf)). Cholesterol is **~45% of raw-material cost** for a producer that buys it in, and Fermenta's own cholesterol plant at Dahej was expected to cut that cost by 20–25%, worth ~300 bp of gross margin ([BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf)). Garden's proprietary oxidation-reduction route adds a yield advantage: **46.9% overall D3 yield versus ~30%** for the older bromination route ([Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). The trough results show what that is worth: Garden's D3 segment still earned a **47.9% gross margin in 2023**, when feed D3 traded at ~RMB 50–56/kg, while Fermenta's EBITDA was negative in both FY23 (−₹39.7 cr) and FY24 (−₹44.5 cr) ([Lianhe Ratings](https://www.lhratings.com/reports/B0648-P66511-2022-GZ2024.pdf); [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [CARE, Nov 2023](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202311131111_Fermenta_Biotech_Limited.pdf); [CARE, Oct 2025](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202510131020_Fermenta_Biotech_Limited.pdf)). European cholesterol looks cost-disadvantaged too (analyst inference, based on [Dishman Q3FY25](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/5fbfa54d-d4f0-43bb-a800-0853fc2d4b39.pdf)). Dishman cut its "Cholesterol SF" sales because they lost money as wool prices escalated through December 2024 ([Dishman Q3FY25](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/5fbfa54d-d4f0-43bb-a800-0853fc2d4b39.pdf)). Cholesterol rose ~20% in early 2025, and Garden blamed part of its Q3 2026 margin squeeze on raw materials ([Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/888396671_135357); [Gelonghui](https://m.gelonghui.com/p/2089901); [Jiemian, Oct 2026](https://www.jiemian.com/article/15214002.html)). At Garden's yields, actual D3 output needs only ~180–300 t of cholesterol a year (calculation: 85–135 t of pure D3 ÷ 46.9% overall yield = 181–288 t; yield from [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); output range from [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf) and [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). Most NF cholesterol therefore goes to other uses such as cosmetics, shrimp feed and lipid nanoparticles, so D3 makers that buy cholesterol compete for it with other users and depend on their largest D3 rival for supply (analyst inference, based on [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2026](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225257262.PDF) on shrimp-feed cholesterol; [Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF) on cosmetics; [Evonik](https://www.evonik.com/en/products/hc/pr_52050742.html) on lipid nanoparticles; [Sina, Jul 2024](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/relnews/cn/2024-07-06/doc-inccemmq1357995.shtml), which says Garden sells most of its lanolin cholesterol to other D3 producers).

This is where a phytosterol route changes the economics (analyst inference, based on [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf)). Symbiotec already buys soy phytosterols at scale, partly from the US and from large food processors such as Cargill, and ferments them in-house into steroid starting materials ([Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf)). A plant route to cholesterol avoids Garden's chokepoint entirely (analyst inference, based on [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF)). The catch is that no published cost curve compares phytosterol-route and lanolin-route D3 (not found in sources searched to Oct 2026; the only yield data located are the patent examples in [Google Patents, EP3906247](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3906247A1/en)). The only yield data come from Fermenta's patent examples: 97% for the cholesterol-forming step and 54% across bromination, elimination and hydrolysis to 7-DHC ([Google Patents, EP3906247](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3906247A1/en)). Trade policy offers little protection (analyst inference, based on [Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/india-imposes-anti-dumping-duty-on-imports-of-vitamin-a-palmitate-from-china-eu-and-switzerland-1421207); [ISID](https://isid.org.in/pdf/WP239.pdf)). No current Indian anti-dumping duty on D3 was found, although the 2025 duty on vitamin A palmitate shows the trade-remedies authority (DGTR) will act on Chinese vitamins when a domestic producer files a case ([Taxscan](https://www.taxscan.in/top-stories/india-imposes-anti-dumping-duty-on-imports-of-vitamin-a-palmitate-from-china-eu-and-switzerland-1421207); D3 duty not found in sources searched to Oct 2026; the DGTR point is analyst inference). D3 was not found among the 41 products in the bulk-drug production-linked incentive (PLI) scheme ([ISID](https://isid.org.in/pdf/WP239.pdf); [DoP PLI press note](https://pharma-dept.gov.in/sites/default/files/Press%20Note%20PLIBD_0_1.pdf)).

## Symbiotec arrives third in a niche smaller than its pitch

### What Symbiotec has and has not said

Symbiotec has been listed since 1 September 2026 ([5paisa](https://www.5paisa.com/blog/symbiotec-pharmalab-ipo-listing)). It reported FY26 revenue of **₹869 cr and EBITDA of ₹238 cr (27.4%)**, and holds an estimated 38% of global corticosteroid API volume ([ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf); [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). Its RHP says it intends to introduce "speciality ingredients such as Vegan Vitamin D3 and Algal based Docosa Hexanoic Acid (DHA)", and to extend into "steroidal derivatives such as vitamin D2 and D3 using our photochemistry capabilities" ([Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). Its nutraceutical website lists vegan cholecalciferol as "Developed" at 40 MIU/g, compliant with the Ph.Eur. monograph, under the tagline "The only plant path to the pharma ceiling" ([nutra.symbiotec.com](https://nutra.symbiotec.com/)). It cites "phytosterols through a 3-stage bioprocess", lists a "developed" yeast-derived D2, and promises a "2026 Nutra Launch" ([nutra.symbiotec.com](https://nutra.symbiotec.com/)). ICICI Direct, citing management, says vegan D3 is "a niche market valued at US$ 600 million and is growing at ~10%" with "few competitors", and that a **dedicated capacity is expected to be functional in FY28** ([ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf)). Several things are absent: a text search of the RHP finds no mention of 7-DHC, calcifediol, calcitriol or lanolin ([Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); analyst full-text search of the RHP). No D3 capacity in tonnes, capex, customer, joint venture or regulatory filing has been disclosed ([Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf); [FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs); not found in other sources searched to Oct 2026). Q1 FY27 commentary focused on biotech contract manufacturing (CDMO) and injectables, and did not mention vitamin D ([Arthneeti](https://www.arthneeti.com/insights/symbiotec-pharma-q1-fy27-earnings); [Sahi](https://www.sahi.com/news/symbiotec-pharmalab-targets-20-25-fy27-growth-anticipating-h2-cdmo-revenue)).

### The technical fit is real and closest to Kerui, not Fermenta

Symbiotec's process begins with "bioconversion of phytosterol to … bisnor alcohol, 9-hydroxy androstenedione or androstenedione" ([Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). It then uses chemistry that includes bromination and debromination, Grignard reactions and hydrogenation ([Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). Its R&D includes a photoreactor, and it runs continuous-flow production for progesterone ([Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). Bisnor alcohol (BA) matters because it is a 22-carbon intermediate that keeps a side-chain handle, which can be extended back to cholesterol's 27-carbon skeleton ([Microbial Cell Factories, 2017](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12934-017-0705-x) on BA as a C22 side-chain product; the extension to C27 is analyst inference by analogy with the C22-alcohol-plus-Grignard step in [Google Patents, EP3906247](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3906247A1/en)). From cholesterol, allylic bromination gives 7-DHC, and UV photolysis followed by thermal isomerisation gives D3 ([Google Patents, EP3906247](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3906247A1/en) for the bromination and photolysis steps; thermal isomerisation is standard secosteroid chemistry, not separately sourced). Every step resembles chemistry Symbiotec already runs (analyst inference, based on [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). The likely route, combining in-house fermentation with chemistry, therefore mirrors Kerui's "fermentation plus multi-step synthesis" more closely than Fermenta's patented route from crude phytosterol, which uses ozonolysis and Grignard chemistry (analyst inference, based on [Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF); [Google Patents, EP3906247](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3906247A1/en); [nutra.symbiotec.com](https://nutra.symbiotec.com/)). This is an inference; Symbiotec has not disclosed its route ([nutra.symbiotec.com](https://nutra.symbiotec.com/), which says only "phytosterols through a 3-stage bioprocess"; [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). What is genuinely new for Symbiotec is scaling up photoreactors, handling D3 (a light- and air-sensitive secosteroid), building D3-specific DMF, CEP and FSSAI dossiers, and checking freedom to operate against Fermenta's European application and its Indian process patent, granted in September 2025 according to Fermenta's annual report (analyst inference, based on [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); [FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs); [Google Patents, EP3906247](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3906247A1/en); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf)). Two points work in its favour: its Ujjain fermentation site already holds FSSAI approval and FSSC accreditation ([Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). And under India's fortification rules, vitamin D may be added to milk and edible oil voluntarily only from plant sources ([ICRIER PB55](https://www.icrier.org/pdf/pb55_Towards-Vitamin-D-Kuposhan-Mukt-Bharat-through-Food-Fortification-FSSAI.pdf)).

### The economics hinge on a premium nobody publishes

Scale is the first problem with the management figure: one tonne of pure D3 holds 4×10¹³ IU, enough for **8 billion 5,000 IU softgels**, or about 22 million people taking one daily for a year (calculation: 10^6 g × 40 MIU/g = 4×10^13 IU ÷ 5,000 IU = 8×10^9 softgels ÷ 365 = 21.9 million person-years; 40 MIU/g definition from [Garden Bio H1 2026 report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-15/1225473446.PDF)). Even modest phytosterol-route capacity is therefore large against vegan demand (analyst inference, based on the calculation above and [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/biotech/fermenta-biotech-to-invest-rs-110-crore-for-dahej-plant-expansion-18512) for Fermenta's ~1 t/yr line). Revenue per tonne depends entirely on price: at IndexBox's modelled pharma crystal price of USD 800–1,500/kg, a 1 t line earns only **₹7–13 cr** a year (calculation: 1,000 kg × USD 800–1,500/kg × ₹88/USD, an FX assumption, = ₹7.0–13.2 cr; price from [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/)). At USD 5,000–10,000/kg, a pure-D3-equivalent price between commodity food-grade powder and stabilised beadlets on a per-IU basis, it earns ₹44–88 cr, or 5–10% of Symbiotec's FY26 revenue (calculation: 1,000 kg × USD 5,000–10,000/kg × ₹88/USD = ₹44–88 cr, and ÷ ₹869 cr = 5–10%; per-IU benchmarks: IndexBox's USD 10–18/kg powder and USD 35–55/kg beadlets at 100,000 IU/g × 400 = USD 4,000–7,200/kg and USD 14,000–22,000/kg of pure-D3 equivalent, from [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/); [ChemAnalyst, Jun 2025](https://www.chemanalyst.com/NewsAndDeals/NewsDetails/rising-demand-and-lean-inventories-push-vitamin-d3-prices-higher-in-china-and-us-37514) implies USD 5,300/kg; revenue from [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf)). Lichen-oil price parity (USD 60,000–120,000/kg of pure-D3 equivalent) would imply USD 60–120m per tonne (calculation: IndexBox's USD 150–300/kg for 100,000 IU/g lichen oil × 400 = USD 60,000–120,000/kg of pure D3, × 1,000 kg = USD 60–120m; [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/)). That is close to the entire human-grade D3 market of ~USD 130–140m, so the lichen premium cannot survive once tonne-scale phytosterol supply arrives (analyst inference, based on the human-grade estimate under "Volume and value split", from [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf) and [BP Equities](https://s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bpwealth-cdn/pdf/1618577883-BP%20Equities_%20Fermenta%20Biotech%20Ltd._Initiating%20Coverage_BUY_Trgt%20402.pdf)). On the same arithmetic, management's US$600m niche would need 5–10 t of pure vegan D3 sold at lichen-oil prices, which is more than lichen can supply (calculation: USD 600m ÷ USD 60–120m per tonne = 5–10 t; at 0.67–2.04 µg/g, 5 t would need ~2.5–7.5 million t of dry lichen; inputs from [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf); [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/); [Lund University](https://www.lunduniversity.lu.se/lup/publication/f49ca442-2470-4d4e-95d1-1af92d805d5c)). It is also larger than the whole producer-level D3 market of ~USD 230–400m, so it can only be a finished-product retail figure (analyst inference, based on the producer-level calculation under "Volume and value split" and [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf)). Fermenta's own spending gives the best clue to realistic pricing: it is investing ~₹60 cr for ~1 t of plant D3 plus 50 kg of derivatives, and at commodity crystal prices that line would earn back its capex only after 5–9 years of revenue ([Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/biotech/fermenta-biotech-to-invest-rs-110-crore-for-dahej-plant-expansion-18512); calculation: ₹60 cr ÷ ₹7.0–13.2 cr of annual revenue at USD 800–1,500/kg = 4.5–8.6 years). Fermenta must therefore expect either a multiple of commodity pricing or calcifediol to carry the investment (analyst inference, based on [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf)). The real-world ceiling so far is Kerui's subsidiary Chundi, the most established plant-D player, with ~USD 10m of revenue in 2023 ([PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946); calculation: RMB 71.6m ÷ RMB 7.2/USD, an FX assumption, = USD 9.9m; "ceiling" and "most established" are analyst inference). Its parent's specialty-intermediates sales then fell in 2025 ([Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF)).

Timing pushes any contribution further out: if capacity arrives in FY28, customer audits, stability data and CEP/DMF or FSSAI approvals typically take 12–36 months after validation batches (analyst inference, not directly sourced; for comparison, Fermenta began commercial-scale plant-D3 production in 2025 and received FSSAI approval in July 2026, per [vegconomist](https://vegconomist.com/ingredients/fermenta-biotech-receives-indian-approval-plant-based-vitamin-d3-ingredient/) and [Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf); capacity date from [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf)). That points to meaningful revenue no earlier than FY29 (analyst inference, based on [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf)). ICICI's FY29E estimate of ₹542 cr EBITDA treats vegan D3 as one of several small new APIs inside an 8–10% API growth rate ([ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf)). Vegan D3 also competes for management attention with ~₹1,475 cr of gross capex, only ~40% of which earns revenue today ([ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf)). That capex is for CDMO (alternative protein, insulin, GLP-1) and double-chamber vials, and its start-up costs pulled Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin down to 21% ([ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf); [Arthneeti](https://www.arthneeti.com/insights/symbiotec-pharma-q1-fy27-earnings)). The strongest Symbiotec-specific positive is policy (analyst inference, based on [ICRIER PB55](https://www.icrier.org/pdf/pb55_Towards-Vitamin-D-Kuposhan-Mukt-Bharat-through-Food-Fortification-FSSAI.pdf)). If FSSAI makes plant-source vitamin D fortification of milk and oil mandatory, India becomes a protected domestic market for plant-source D3, though its size in tonnes cannot be estimated from these sources because FSSAI dose levels and fortified volumes were not retrieved (analyst inference, based on [ICRIER PB55](https://www.icrier.org/pdf/pb55_Towards-Vitamin-D-Kuposhan-Mukt-Bharat-through-Food-Fortification-FSSAI.pdf)). A 2020 report said a mandate was months away, but a 2025 policy brief still describes fortification as voluntary ([NutraIngredients-Asia](https://nutraingredients-asia.com/Article/2020/09/22/Mandatory-fortification-in-India-Regulation-set-to-compel-firms-to-fortify-edible-oil-and-milk-FSSAI-exclusive); [ICRIER PB55](https://www.icrier.org/pdf/pb55_Towards-Vitamin-D-Kuposhan-Mukt-Bharat-through-Food-Fortification-FSSAI.pdf)).

## Conclusion

For Symbiotec, the question is pricing, not capacity or Chinese cost (analyst inference, based on [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/); [Mysteel](https://list1.m.mysteel.com/zhishi/slwssD3jg.html)). Its phytosterol route avoids Garden's cholesterol chokepoint and the feed-grade price cycle altogether, so the RMB 95/kg feed price is irrelevant to it (analyst inference, based on [Tianfeng Securities](http://pdf.dfcfw.com/pdf/H3_AP202412161641320358_1.pdf); [Mysteel](https://list1.m.mysteel.com/zhishi/slwssD3jg.html); [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf)). What matters is whether "plant-origin, pharma-grade crystal" holds a lasting premium (analyst inference, based on [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/); [OneGreenPlanet](https://www.onegreenplanet.org/natural-health/lichen-vs-lanolin-why-the-source-of-your-vitamin-d3-matters-in-2026/)). That creates a paradox, because the scalability that makes phytosterol D3 viable is what destroys the scarcity premium lichen D3 enjoys: Chundi's reported 10 t line, Fermenta's 1 t and any Symbiotec line together could supply billions of doses into a market where ingredient value is measured in tens of millions of dollars (analyst inference, based on [SINOQUAL](https://www.halalkosher.com/zh-CN/articles/legacy-39); [Indian Pharma Post](https://www.indianpharmapost.com/biotech/fermenta-biotech-to-invest-rs-110-crore-for-dahej-plant-expansion-18512); [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946); [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/); calculation: 11 t × 8 billion 5,000 IU doses per tonne = ~88 billion doses). The empirical ceiling so far, Chundi at ~USD 10m of revenue with its parent's specialty sales shrinking in 2025, fits that view better than management's US$600m does ([PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946); [Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF); [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf); the comparison is analyst inference). Analysts should value vegan D3 at zero in base estimates (analyst inference, based on [ICICI Direct](https://mailcontent.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_symbiotec_convictionidea.pdf); [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/); [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946)). The triggers to revisit are a disclosed tonnage and capex, a D3 CEP, DMF or FSSAI filing, Fermenta's first published VITADEE Green prices and orders, and a mandatory-fortification notification (analyst inference, based on [Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); [FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs); [Fermenta Q1FY27](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Investor-PresentationQ1FY27.pdf); [ICRIER PB55](https://www.icrier.org/pdf/pb55_Towards-Vitamin-D-Kuposhan-Mukt-Bharat-through-Food-Fortification-FSSAI.pdf)).

Symbiotec's real advantages are in-house fermentation of BA from soy sterols, independent of Chinese lanolin, plus a regulatory track record of 43 DMFs and 23 CEPs ([Symbiotec RHP](https://assets.upstox.com/ipo/documents/rhp/symbiotec-rhp.pdf); "real advantages" is analyst inference). These are worth more on high-value, kilogram-scale secosteroids than on vegan D3 tonnage (analyst inference, based on [Dr. Reddy's 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001135951/000119312514262694/d756631d6k.htm); [Investing.com](https://ng.investing.com/news/company-news/faes-farma-fy-2025-slides-23-revenue-surge-masks-profit-pressures-93CH-2367801); [OPKO 10-K](https://www.opko.com/investors/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0001193125-26-076596/opk-20251231.htm); [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/search/vitamin-d-ingredients-price-evidence-world-2026/)). Calcifediol has only three valid CEP holders while Garden, Fermenta and Kerui all move toward it ([EDQM calcifediol](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/1295); [Garden Bio IR, Apr 2025](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-07/1223021870.PDF); [Fermenta AR FY26](https://fermentabiotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Annual-Report-2025-26-1.pdf); [PopCJ](https://www.popcj.com/depth/0642502404363946)). Plant cholesterol has a separate pharma-excipient market in lipid nanoparticles, where Evonik is the benchmark; Kerui currently sells its high-purity plant cholesterol mainly into cosmetics ([Evonik](https://www.evonik.com/en/products/hc/pr_52050742.html); [Ang Likang 2025 AR](https://disc.static.szse.cn/download/disc/disk03/finalpage/2026-04-29/d1db6d43-291e-489d-a3b0-d99559193f23.PDF)). The DSM–CVC separation, closing at end-2026, is likely to reopen Western human-nutrition supply contracts in 2027 ([dsm-firmenich, Feb 2026](https://our-company.dsm-firmenich.com/en/our-company/news/press-releases/2026/dsm-firmenich-announces-agreement-to-divest-animal-nutrition-and-health-to-cvc-capital-partners.html); [ThePoultrySite](https://www.thepoultrysite.com/news/2026/02/dsm-firmenich-announces-agreement-to-divest-animal-nutrition-health-to-cvc-capital-partners); the 2027 renegotiation is analyst inference). A non-Chinese supplier holding a CEP would matter in that window (analyst inference, based on [EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72)). Fermenta is ready for it; Symbiotec is not yet (analyst inference, based on [EDQM cholecalciferol CEPs](https://extranet.edqm.eu/4DLink1/4DCGI/Web_View/mono/72); [FDA DMF list](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-master-files-dmfs/list-drug-master-files-dmfs)).

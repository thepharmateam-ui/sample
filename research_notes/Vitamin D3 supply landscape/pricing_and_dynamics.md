# Vitamin D3 Pricing History and Market Dynamics, 2015 to October 2026

Scope: feed-grade vitamin D3 (500,000 IU/g, "VD3 500") price history, other grades, feedstock (lanolin / wool grease, NF cholesterol, 7-DHC) and cost structure, company financials as evidence of the cycle, trade and policy, and the outlook to 2028. The notes are written for an Indian sell-side analyst judging whether Symbiotec Pharmalab (steroid API maker, Indore) can enter D3 profitably.

Conventions: unless stated otherwise, "RMB/kg" is China's domestic feed-grade 500,000 IU/g price. Producer **list/quoted** prices (报价) and **market/transaction** prices (主流价/成交价) are different things and often far apart; this is flagged wherever it matters. Items marked **[UNVERIFIED]** come from a single secondary source, a search-result summary, or a source with internal inconsistencies.

---

## 1. Feed-grade D3 (500,000 IU/g) price history, 2015 to October 2026, and the reason for each move

### Takeaway
Feed-grade D3 is a highly cyclical, oligopolistic, China-dominated commodity. It spends long stretches near cash cost (about RMB 50-110/kg), interrupted by short supply-shock spikes of 3-10x: 2017-18 (environmental crackdown), H1 2020 (COVID), and H2 2024 to H1 2025 (capacity exits, plant accidents, BASF knock-on, supply discipline). By September-October 2026 the market had fallen back to about RMB 95-105/kg, even though the leader Garden Bio still lists RMB 360/kg.

### Inferences
- **Amplitude.** Trough to peak has been roughly 5-10x: about RMB 50-60 in 2023 to RMB 250 in Q3 2024 and 400-600 ex-works in Nov 2024; about RMB 100 in early 2020 to 500-700 in April 2020; about RMB 250 to 500-600 in 2018.
- **Shape.** Peaks are short, one to three quarters. Troughs are long: mid-2020 to mid-2024 was about four years of mostly sub-RMB 110 prices. Most of the decade sat in a RMB 50-130/kg band.
- **Structural overcapacity.** Nameplate capacity is roughly 1.4-2x demand (12,800-14,740 t vs 6,750-10,836 t). Prices therefore revert towards the cash cost of the marginal Chinese producer once a supply shock clears.
- **Spikes are supply-driven,** not demand-driven: environmental closures, COVID logistics, accidents, BASF knock-on and producer discipline.
- **Transaction prices sit far below list prices.** In Oct 2026, Garden's RMB 360 list versus about RMB 95-105 traded (a 3.5x gap), and in 2018, RMB 1,500 list versus 460-500 market. Analysts should model on market references (Baichuan, Boyar, Zhuochuang, Mysteel), not producer list prices.
- **Where 2026 sits.** At about RMB 95-130/kg in May-Oct 2026, the market is back near the low end of its historical band. It is above the 2023 trough (~50) but well below the 2024-25 spike.

### Gaps
- No reliable dated 2015-2016 price points were found. The 2007-2017 "80-240" band has a unit error in the source.

- No data for Q4 2025 to April 2026 except the qualitative dsm-firmenich commentary.

## 2. Prices of other grades (D3 oil, crystalline/USP/EP, food beadlets, vegan, 25-OH-D3) and how they relate to feed grade

### Takeaway
Pure crystalline D3 (40 MIU/g) is 80x as potent as feed grade, so feed-grade prices translate into a "contained D3" value of about RMB 8,000/kg pure at RMB 100/kg feed. Pharma and food grades carry a premium and are far less cyclical: Fermenta's human-nutrition revenue held up while its feed realisations swung ±40%. 25-OH-D3 sells at about RMB 4,000/kg. Few reliable public price points exist for D3 oil 1 MIU/g, USP crystalline in bulk, or vegan D3.

- **Demand segments.** D3 demand comes from five segments: pharma, dietary supplements, cold-water-dispersible (food), veterinary, and animal feed (feed grade and oil). ### Inferences
- **Potency arithmetic.** 1 kg of crystalline D3 (40 MIU/g) is equivalent to 80 kg of feed grade (500 KIU/g).
  - At RMB 100/kg feed grade, the D3 contained is worth about RMB 8,000/kg pure, roughly USD 1,100 at about RMB 7.1-7.2/USD.
  - At RMB 500/kg feed grade it is worth about RMB 40,000/kg.
  - Against the year-end 2019 pharma price of about RMB 18,000-19,000/kg, pharma crystalline sold at about 2.3x the contained value of feed grade at the trough (~RMB 100). That premium shrinks or reverses during feed-grade spikes, which is why pharma grade behaves as the less cyclical anchor.
  - These numbers combine sources and are indicative only.
- **Grade strategy for an entrant.** Feed grade is a formulation of crystalline D3 (or resin) on a carrier. An entrant with crystalline and pharma capability can choose its grade mix. The economic moat lies in regulated grades (USP/EP/CEP, FSSAI, EU GMP) and derivatives (25-OH-D3, calcifediol API), not in feed grade.

### Gaps

## 3. Feedstock (lanolin / wool grease, NF cholesterol, 7-DHC) and the cost structure of making D3

### Takeaway
D3 is made from NF-grade (≥95%) cholesterol extracted from wool grease (lanolin). Since 2020, China's GB 7300.202-2019 has required lanolin-derived cholesterol for feed D3. NF cholesterol is even more concentrated than D3: Garden Bio holds about 70% of it. Cholesterol is about 45% of a non-integrated producer's raw-material cost. Integration into cholesterol is the decisive cost advantage, which is why Garden earned roughly 48% gross margin on D3 even at the 2023 price trough. No public source gives the cholesterol to 7-DHC to D3 yield or a full cost per kg.

### Inferences
- **Integrated cost.** Garden's D3 and analogues segment kept about 48% gross margin in 2023, when the market averaged about RMB 50-56/kg. That implies an integrated cash cost well below RMB 50/kg feed-grade equivalent. The segment also includes higher-value food grade and 25-OH-D3, so the feed-only margin is lower and undisclosed.
- **Non-integrated cost.** A producer buying NF cholesterol, where cholesterol is about 45% of raw-material cost, would have been near or below breakeven at the trough. Fermenta, though partly integrated, reported negative consolidated EBITDA in FY23-FY24.
- **Feedstock concentration in the entrant's supplier.** Garden supplies about 70% of NF cholesterol and is also the largest D3 producer. A new non-Chinese entrant buying cholesterol would therefore depend on its largest competitor, or on the small non-Chinese pool (Nippon Fine Chemical, NK, the DSM India unit). Wool-grease-to-cholesterol integration, which Fermenta did in FY21, is a prerequisite for viability.
- **Relevance to Symbiotec.** A steroid API maker's sterol chemistry, purification and possibly photochemistry skills are relevant (7-DHC is a sterol and D3 is made by UV photolysis). Feedstock access to wool grease and NF cholesterol is the strategic bottleneck. (This is inference, not sourced to Symbiotec disclosures.)

- No source found for:
  - Cholesterol (NF) price history in USD/kg or RMB/kg, 2015-2026.
  - 7-DHC price.
  - Australian or NZ wool-grease feedstock price series.
  - Process yield from cholesterol to 7-DHC to pre-D3 to D3, the cost of the UV photochemistry step, or an all-in cost per kg.
- Garden, NHU and Fermenta do not disclose D3 unit cost.
- The 2020 and 2024 regulatory milestones under GB 7300.202-2019 (effective date vs enforcement) are described inconsistently across sources.

- **Fermenta.** EBITDA swung from about 37% to about −17% between FY19 and FY24, a 50+ pp range. A smaller, partly integrated, non-Chinese producer with feed exposure shows far more downside volatility than Garden.
- **Implied quarterly EBITDA (inference from ₹22.3 crore being −37% YoY and −10% QoQ).** Q1 FY26 about ₹35 crore, Q4 FY26 about ₹25 crore. The Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin was about 18% (22.3/124.3).
- **Animal-nutrition run-rate.** The Q1 FY27 run-rate is about ₹50 crore a year, against ₹109 crore in FY26. The 2025 feed upcycle has unwound for Fermenta.
- **Evidence the 2024-25 spike was real.** The H1 2025 spike shows up in dsm-firmenich ANH's 19% margin, NHU's 47.8% nutrition gross margin and Fermenta's FY25 recovery. Its unwind in 2026 shows up at all three companies plus Garden.

## 5. Trade flows and policy (China exports, EU and India trade remedies, India PLI, US tariffs)

### Takeaway
China supplies about 82-86% of global vitamins and of D3 capacity. The US is effectively 100% China-dependent for feed D3. US tariffs since 2025 have been messy for D3: sources conflict on whether D3 was exempt from the 2025 reciprocal tariffs, and by March 2026 vitamins were reportedly exempt from the replacement Section 122 surcharge. Garden says the impact is "limited". India has no current anti-dumping duty on D3 (it has one on vitamin A palmitate from 2025, and imposed one on vitamin AD3 500/100 from China in 2002). D3 does not appear among the 41 PLI bulk-drug products, which include vitamins B1 and B6. The EU fined a D3 cartel in 2001.

### Cited Findings
- **China vitamin output and exports (Boyar Hexun via Garden).** China's 2024 vitamin output is estimated at 420,000 t (−3.6%, 85.9% of global). Exports were 332,000 t (+5.6%), worth USD 3.0bn (+11.1%). — Garden Bio 2024 Annual Report ([cninfo](https://www.cninfo.com.cn/new/disclosure/stock?stockCode=300401))

- **D3 exports.** China's D3 export volume rose about 25% YoY in Q1 2025. — [Gelonghui](https://m.gelonghui.com/p/2089901) **[UNVERIFIED; no customs source given]**
- **US dependence.** AFIA told a USTR Section 301 hearing that US D3 supply is 100% China-dependent. Fermenta "operates the entirety of India's D3 capacity at two sites", a small single-digit share of global supply. — [Expana](https://www.expanamarkets.com/insights/article/afia-highlights-non-china-vitamin-and-amino-acid-supply-at-section-301-hearing/) (via search summary; full text not retrievable)

  - Vitamin D3 was not found in the PLI product lists retrieved.
  - Fermenta noted that its Green Chemistry business benefited from Penicillin G manufacturing revived in India under the PLI. — Fermenta Annual Report 2025-26 ([fermentabiotech.com](https://fermentabiotech.com))

### Inferences
- **US tariffs.** Their effect on D3 pricing in 2025-26 looks second-order compared with Chinese supply discipline and the later competitive unwind: D3 prices fell through 2026 despite tariff noise. The conflicting sources may mean HTS-line specifics (D3 under 2936.29) mattered for some products and not others.
- **India protection.** India's vitamin A palmitate duty shows that DGTR will act against Chinese vitamin dumping when a domestic producer petitions; Piramal filed the A-palmitate case. Any Indian D3 entrant, Fermenta or Symbiotec, could in principle seek trade remedies. There is no current D3 duty, and Fermenta now sells Indian feed D3 directly to large feed customers.
- **PLI.** No PLI support for D3 appears to be available under the bulk-drug PLI as constituted.

## 6. Outlook for 2026-2028 (capacity, demand, consensus on prices)

### Takeaway
As of October 2026 the market is in a down-leg: about RMB 95-105/kg traded, against a RMB 360 list price. dsm-firmenich calls D3 "slightly softened" with "new approved sources". Margins are compressing at Garden, Fermenta and dsm-firmenich ANH. The supply side holds large idle capacity, plus delayed Chinese expansions slated for "mid-2026". Demand grows with feed tonnage plus human-nutrition and 25-OH-D3 upgrading. No published consensus price forecast was found. The history suggests a multi-year trough near cash cost, broken only by another supply shock.

### Cited Findings
- **dsm-firmenich Q3 2026 outlook (15 Jun 2026).** D3 prices "slightly softened" on "increased competitive activity and new approved sources, which may gradually align regional prices". The market is "relatively balanced" and supply "appears sufficient to meet demand despite ongoing constraints". "Delayed capacity expansions limit major supply shifts". Freight costs are elevated on Middle East disruption. — [dsm-firmenich Q3 2026 outlook](https://www.dsm-firmenich.com/anh/en_NA/news/articles/vitamin-market-update-q3-2026.html)
- **dsm-firmenich Q1 2026 outlook (15 Dec 2025).** Chinese D3 expansion plans were pushed back to mid-2026, and buyers were purchasing carefully. — [dsm-firmenich Q1 2026 outlook](https://www.dsm-firmenich.com/anh/en_NA/news/articles/vitamin-market-update-q1-2026.html)
- **Planned or new capacity.**
  - Gansu Jiahui Pharma's planned D3 capacity needs environmental approval and may be delayed. — [Gelonghui](https://m.gelonghui.com/p/2089901) **[UNVERIFIED]**
  - Garden's "4,000 t/yr cholecalciferol (D3) rodenticide project" is self-funded; the D3 here is for rodenticide use. — Garden IR record, 7 Apr 2025 ([cninfo](https://www.cninfo.com.cn/new/disclosure/stock?stockCode=300401))

  - Rising health awareness, vitamin D deficiency and online health-product purchasing support human D3 demand in the medium term. — [CARE, Oct 2025](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202510131020_Fermenta_Biotech_Limited.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Base case, 2026-2028.** Feed-grade D3 likely stays in the RMB 80-150/kg band typical of non-shock years, given:
  - nameplate capacity of 12,800-14,740 t against demand of 6,750-10,836 t;
  - Chinese expansions that were delayed rather than cancelled;
  - "new approved sources" (dsm-firmenich);
  - Garden's falling margins despite higher volumes.
  - The 2026 trajectory (RMB 128-132 in May, 97 in September, about 95 in October) points to the lower half of that band.
- **Upside triggers** would be supply shocks like those of 2017-18, 2020 and 2024-25: Chinese environmental or safety closures, a plant accident, feedstock (wool-grease or cholesterol) disruption, or coordinated "production to orders" by Garden and Haisheng. History implies such a spike every 2-5 years, lasting 1-3 quarters.
- **Size of the feed-grade profit pool (rough inference).** 6,750-10,836 t × RMB 100/kg is about RMB 0.7-1.1bn (about USD 95-150m) a year at October 2026 prices. At RMB 250/kg it would be about RMB 1.7-2.7bn. The pool is small and is dominated by an integrated incumbent with about 30-45% D3 share and about 70% of NF cholesterol.
- **Implications for Symbiotec (inference).** A feed-grade-led entry would be a low-return, highly cyclical bet. Fermenta's own strategy shift, from nearly two-thirds feed revenue about six years ago to 20% in FY26 with growth in human nutrition, calcifediol and plant-source D3, suggests the defensible economics are in regulated human/pharma D3 API, D3 analogues (25-OH-D3, calcifediol, calcitriol-type actives) and India-specific supply. Backward integration into wool grease and NF cholesterol, or a secure non-Chinese cholesterol source, is a precondition.

- The identity of the "new approved sources" dsm-firmenich mentions (for example, new EU-registered or US-registered Chinese or Indian suppliers) was not established.

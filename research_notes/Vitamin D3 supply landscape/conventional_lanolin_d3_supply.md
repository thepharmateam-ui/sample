# Global Supply Side of Conventional (Lanolin/Cholesterol-Derived) Vitamin D3 — Fact Base as of Oct 2026

Unit conventions used throughout (important — almost all Chinese/industry "tonnes" are commercial-form tonnes, not pure D3):
- Pure/crystalline D3 = 40 MIU/g (Garden Bio's own definitions: "维生素D3结晶 = 含4000万I.U./g的纯品"; "1μg = 40 IU") — [Garden Bio H1 2026 report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-15/1225473446.PDF)
- Feed-grade D3 powder = 500,000 IU/g ("饲料级维生素D3粉 = 含50万I.U./g"); food-grade powder = 100,000–1,000,000 IU/g — [Garden Bio H1 2026 report](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-08-15/1225473446.PDF)
- Conversion (my arithmetic): 1 t of 500 KIU/g powder = 12.5 kg pure D3 (80 t powder ≈ 1 t crystal); 1 t of 1 MIU/g oil = 25 kg pure D3 (40 t oil ≈ 1 t crystal).
- Industry capacity tables (Tianfeng, BP Equities/Boyar, Kingdomway) are expressed on a 500 KIU/g-equivalent basis unless noted.

## 1. Global capacity, actual output and demand (with unit conversions)

### Takeaway
Global nameplate capacity is ~14,600–14,740 t/yr of 500 KIU/g-equivalent D3 (≈182–184 t of pure crystalline D3), of which China holds ~83–90%; actual demand/output is far lower (~6,750 t 500 KIU/g-eq ≈ 84 t pure in 2019 per Boyar; a theoretical feed-requirement estimate gives ~10,836 t ≈ 135 t pure), so the industry runs at roughly 45–75% utilisation and is structurally over-capacitied, with price driven by producer discipline rather than physical scarcity.

- China total vitamin context (all vitamins, Boyar): 2024 China vitamin output 420 kt (85.9% of global); 2025 491 kt (+5.2%, 89% of global); 2025 China vitamin exports 374 kt (+8.3%), US$3.6 bn — [Garden Bio 2024 AR](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-03-25/1222880975.PDF); [Garden Bio 2025 AR](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-18/1225116031.PDF)

**Unit conversions applied to headline numbers (my arithmetic, 1 t 500 KIU/g = 12.5 kg pure)**
- 14,740 t capacity ≈ 184 t pure D3; 14,600 t ≈ 182.5 t; China 13,100 t ≈ 164 t pure
- Demand 6,750 t ≈ 84 t pure; theoretical 10,836 t ≈ 135 t pure; China 2019 output 5,650 t ≈ 71 t pure; China 2019 exports 4,050 t ≈ 51 t pure
- In 1 MIU/g oil terms: 184 t pure ≈ 7,370 t of oil-equivalent

### Inferences
- Implied 2019 utilisation: China ~43% (5,650/13,100); global ~46% (6,750/14,600). Even using the higher theoretical demand (10,836 t), nameplate exceeds requirement by ~35%. Capacity is not the binding constraint; price spikes come from coordinated output restraint, environmental shutdowns, accidents, or feedstock (cholesterol/7-DHC) bottlenecks.
- Total world D3 is a tiny molecule market by mass (~85–135 t pure/yr of actual consumption), so a single mid-sized steroid-chemistry plant producing 10–20 t/yr of crystal would be equivalent to ~6–12% of global supply — relevant to any Symbiotec entry thesis.
- Capacity figures conflate feed/food powders and oils converted to a 500 KIU/g basis; pure-crystal capacity per producer is almost never disclosed. The one explicit crystal disclosure found is Garden Bio's 18 t/yr "胆钙化醇" (cholecalciferol) project (see Section 2).

### Gaps
- No reliable published figure for actual global D3 output in 2023, 2024 or 2025 (only 2019 Boyar data). Boyar (博亚和讯), Baiinfo (百川盈孚) and SCI (卓创) hold this behind paywalls.
- No producer-level crystal (40 MIU/g) capacity except Garden Bio's 18 t project; Fermenta's crystal capacity in tonnes is undisclosed.
- Demand estimates conflict (6,750 t vs 10,836 t); the latter is a theoretical recommended-dose calculation, not observed consumption.

## 2. Supplier-by-supplier profiles (capacity, integration, grades, recent changes)

### Takeaway
Supply is a Chinese oligopoly led by Zhejiang Garden Bio (~28% of capacity, 30–40% claimed share, the only fully integrated lanolin→cholesterol→7-DHC→D3→25-OH-D3 player), followed by Taizhou Hisound, NHU, Kingdomway and Zhejiang Medicine; outside China the meaningful players are DSM-Firmenich (in-house D3 reportedly curtailed 2023–25 and now buying from Garden, with the vitamins business being carved out to CVC) and Fermenta (India; pharma/food-grade niche with ~25% human-API share and in-house cholesterol); Dishman/Carbogen Amcis (Veenendaal) is a cholesterol and vitamin-D-analogue player rather than a bulk D3 maker; BASF's current D3 synthesis status is unverified.

## 3. Lanolin / cholesterol feedstock

### Takeaway
NF-grade (≥95%) lanolin cholesterol is the true chokepoint: global capacity is only ~1,800–2,000 t/yr, of which Garden Bio holds ~1,200 t (>50–70% share), with the rest in Japan (Nippon Fine Chemical), Singapore (Enkay/NK), and Dishman (Netherlands/India), and Fermenta captive in India; crude wool grease itself is plentiful (~24 kt/yr lanolin market; China and Australia are the biggest wool producers), so integration into cholesterol, not lanolin access, is the source of cost advantage.

## 4. Market concentration, China's share, exports, and antitrust history

### Takeaway
China holds ~83% of global D3 capacity (≈96% if DSM's ~2,000 t is excluded) and exported ~72% of its 2019 output; capacity HHI is ~1,740 (rising to ~2,090 ex-DSM), while SAMR's 2019 review found output-share HHIs of ~2,240–2,800 (pre-JV) for feed and human D3 markets — a concentrated oligopoly with a documented cartel history (EU 2001 D3 cartel) and a 2019 Chinese merger-control remedy on the Garden–DSM 7-DHC JV that lapsed in late 2024.

## 5. Key events and price history (2014–Oct 2026)

### Takeaway
D3 is a violently cyclical micro-market: feed-grade prices ran ~RMB 50–90/kg at troughs (2022–23) and spiked 3–8x in supply shocks (2017–18 environmental crackdown; 2024–25 output discipline plus DSM retreat, reaching RMB 600–660/kg in Apr–mid 2025), before collapsing back to ~RMB 135–160/kg by Apr–May 2026 as new Chinese capacity and restored overseas supply arrived; producers' realisations fell ~40% YoY in mid-2026.

### Inferences
- The price path implies the 2024–25 spike was mainly supply discipline + DSM's retreat + Kingdomway's outage on top of an over-capacitied base; once delayed Chinese additions began arriving and restraint broke down (2H25–2026), prices fell ~75% from peak (RMB ~600 → ~140/kg), even as Garden's volumes rose and its feedstock costs increased (margin squeeze visible in Q3 2026).
- At ~RMB 140/kg (≈US$19–20/kg) for 500 KIU/g powder, the implied value of pure D3 is ~RMB 11,000/kg (≈US$1,550/kg) — my arithmetic (×80). For a new entrant, feed-grade economics at trough prices are thin; pharma/food-grade (CEP/USP) niches are where non-Chinese players (Fermenta, DSM) have survived.
- The BASF 2017 citral fire is widely cited for vitamin A/E, but available sources attribute the 2017–18 D3 spike to Chinese environmental (Blue Skies) shutdowns rather than BASF.

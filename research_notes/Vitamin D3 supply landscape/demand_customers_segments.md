# Vitamin D3 demand by end-use segment, key customers, and the pharma vitamin D analogue API market (as of Oct 2026)

Conversion conventions in these notes: 1 g pure cholecalciferol = 40 MIU. Feed-grade "D3 500" (500,000 IU/g) holds 1.25% pure D3, so 1 t of D3 500 = 12.5 kg pure D3. "D3 100" (100,000 IU/g, the usual food/supplement beadlet or spray-dried form) holds 0.25% pure D3, so 1 t = 2.5 kg pure D3. Chinese industry tonnages are normally quoted on a D3 500 (500,000 IU/g) basis. Where a source does not state its basis, that is flagged. All pure-D3 conversions below are my arithmetic, not the source's.

## 1. Global D3 demand volume and value by segment (feed vs human nutrition vs pharma)

### Takeaway
On the most grounded estimates (Chinese brokers and industry data compilers), global D3 demand is about 6,750 to 10,800 t a year on a D3 500 basis, which is roughly 85 to 135 t of pure D3. Feed takes about 75 to 90% of that volume. Value splits very differently. Feed-grade D3 is a volatile commodity, while human and pharma grades sell at stable, higher per-IU prices, so human nutrition plus pharma is probably about half of producer-level value or more, even though it is only 10 to 25% of volume. Credible producer-level market value is about USD 300 to 600m. The "USD 1.7 to 4.2bn" figures in market-research reports almost certainly include downstream formulated value and should not be used as an ingredient market size.

### Inferences
- **Volume reconciliation.** The figures converge on roughly 7,000 to 9,000 t of D3 500-equivalent actual demand in 2023-24, which is about 90 to 115 t of pure D3.
  - The 10,836 t (Tianfeng, 8.4 g/t) is an upper-bound feed assumption.
  - The 14,796 t is theoretical need, not consumption.
  - Feed is about 75 to 90% (70 to 100 t pure).
  - Human nutrition plus pharma is about 10 to 25% (about 10 to 25 t pure).
  - The 90/6/4 split is China's domestic structure, which is feed-heavy. The global non-feed share is likely higher because US/EU supplement use is concentrated outside China. The older 7,250 t / 5,500 t feed split implies about 24% non-feed globally.
- **Scale check on pharma volume.** 1 kg of pure D3 = 40 billion IU = about 667,000 doses of 60,000 IU, so 1 t of pure D3 is about 667 million 60K doses. Even a very large Indian high-dose market of several hundred million units a year therefore consumes well under 1 t of pure D3. Pharma D3 is tiny in tonnes but high in value per kg.
- **Value reconciliation (producer level).**
  - Feed: about 7,000 to 7,700 t of D3 500 × RMB 56 to 250/kg (the 2024 price range) gives about RMB 0.4 to 1.9bn, or USD 55 to 265m, depending on the price cycle.
  - Human grades: Fermenta's FY26 human D3 revenue of ₹293 cr (about USD 33 to 35m at roughly ₹85 to 88/USD, my FX assumption), with its self-claimed ~25% share (2021 vintage), implies a human D3 API/ingredient market of about USD 130 to 150m.
  - Total producer-level value is therefore about USD 300 to 600m. That is consistent with the Market.us (USD 337m) and Market Reports World (USD 748m) figures, and inconsistent with the USD 1.7 to 4.2bn reports, which probably include formulated or finished products.
  - In trough feed-price years (2022-23, feed D3 around RMB 50 to 90/kg), human and pharma grades could be more than 60% of value. At 2024-25 peak feed prices, feed may approach 50%.
- **Fermenta's own mix shows the value tilt.** Human nutrition is about 73% of Fermenta's D3 revenue (₹293 of ₹402 cr in FY26), even though it is a minority of tonnage.

### Gaps
- No primary global segment split (feed/supplement/food/pharma) was found in volume terms from DSM-Firmenich, BASF or an independent body. All volume splits trace back to Chinese data compilers (Boyar Hexun, Guanyan, Huajing) or to feed × inclusion-rate arithmetic.
- No reliable global tonnage for the supplement segment alone (as distinct from food fortification and pharma) was found.
- Fermenta's tonnage sold is not disclosed. The ~25% human-API share claim dates from 2021 and is company-sourced.

## 2. Nutraceutical and food customers: who buys D3 and how they source it

### Takeaway
No public source names the D3 suppliers of Haleon (Centrum, Caltrate), Bayer (One A Day, Supradyn), Nestlé Health Science (Nature's Bounty, Pure Encapsulations), Pharmavite (Nature Made), Church & Dwight (Vitafusion), Amway/Nutrilite, Herbalife, By-health, H&H/Swisse, Costco/Kirkland, Abbott or Danone. What the evidence does show is the channel structure:
- Chinese crystal and NF-cholesterol makers, Garden above all, sell much of their output to the big formulators/premixers (DSM-Firmenich, BASF, Trouw/Nutreco, Cargill, Adisseo) under long-term agreements.
- DSM-Firmenich and BASF hold DMFs and CEPs for formulated D3 forms used by supplement and pharma makers.
- Fermenta sells crystalline, oily and spray-dried D3 to a fragmented base of 400+ pharma, supplement and food customers.
- Kingdomway is vertically integrated into a consumer brand (Doctor's Best).

  - Garden's 25-OH-D3 is "mainly supplied to DSM" at stable prices. The DSM agreements run 10 to 15 years — [Sina, 7 Jul 2024](https://finance.sina.cn/2024-07-07/detail-incchusi2216655.d.html); [Sina, 6 Jul 2024](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/relnews/cn/2024-07-06/doc-inccemmq1357995.shtml)

### Inferences
- The likely sourcing pattern for the large Western brands and CDMOs is formulated D3 (beadlets, oils, CWS/SD powders) bought from DSM-Firmenich, BASF and Fermenta, plus some Chinese formulators such as NHU, Kingdomway and Garden food-grade. This is because supplement and pharma tablet/softgel makers need pharmacopoeial forms with DMF/CEP support (see Q4). Direct buying of crystal from Chinese makers appears more common among premixers and formulators than among brand owners. This is inferred from the customer lists above, not confirmed for any named brand.
- Fermenta's concentration data (top customer 9%) suggests it sells to many mid-sized supplement and pharma formulators rather than relying on one or two global brand owners.
- The DSM-Firmenich ANH carve-out (closing around end-2026) puts DSM's vitamin production, including D3, into a CVC-controlled "Essential Products Company". DSM-Firmenich's human-nutrition side becomes a long-term buyer. Supply relationships to human-nutrition customers may be renegotiated in 2027.

### Gaps
- No source confirmed the D3 supplier or sourcing route for any named brand owner: Haleon, Bayer, Nestlé Health Science, Pharmavite, Church & Dwight, Amway, Herbalife, By-health, H&H/Swisse, Costco/Kirkland, Abbott (Ensure, Similac) or Danone. These are typically confidential. Supplier-qualification lists, import/export bill-of-lading data (e.g., Panjiva, ImportGenius) or trade-press interviews would be needed.
- No US vitamin D category dollar figure for 2025 (SPINS, Circana, NBJ) was found in public sources.

## 3. Pharma customers: D3 in Rx/OTC drug products (India high-dose, US/EU Rx)

### Takeaway
India is the standout pharma-D3 market. Cholecalciferol 60,000 IU (capsules, sachets/granules, tablets, oral solutions, injections) dominates branded cholecalciferol, with hundreds of brands from Alkem, Cadila Pharmaceuticals, USV, Eris, Cipla, Aristo and others. The market was ₹5.38bn in 2018 (latest hard figure found); current IQVIA molecule-level numbers are not public. In volume it is a sub-tonne pure-D3 market. In the EU, licensed high-dose D3 medicines exist, such as Dekristol 20,000/25,000 IU and Fultium-D3 20,000 IU. In the US the classic Rx vitamin D (Drisdol) is ergocalciferol (D2), not D3.

### Inferences
- **India's volume is small.** If India sells on the order of 200 to 500 million 60K-equivalent doses a year (my assumption; no data found), that is about 0.3 to 0.75 t of pure D3, under 1% of global D3 tonnage. The value is high: at ₹20 to 40 per 60K unit, finished value is roughly ₹400 to 2,000 cr. API cost is a trivial share of finished-dose value.
- The shift from calcitriol and alfacalcidol (2013) to cholecalciferol 60K (2020) has reduced Indian demand for analogue APIs relative to D3. Calcitriol still had the most products in 2020 (502), mostly calcium combinations, so India remains a meaningful calcitriol and alfacalcidol formulation market.
- NPPA price control on D3 (but not D2) caps branded D3 pricing. That pushes Indian formulators toward low-cost API, from Chinese crystal or Fermenta.

### Gaps
- No IQVIA, AWACS or Pharmarack molecule-level value or volume for cholecalciferol in India (2024-26) was found publicly. - The share of global D3 going into licensed drug products (vs supplements) is not quantified anywhere found.

## 4. Pharma-grade D3 and vitamin D analogue APIs: CEP/DMF holders, analogue markets, chemistry

### Takeaway
Pharma-grade cholecalciferol is an oligopoly at the regulatory level:
- **EDQM CEPs** (valid): DSM-Firmenich, Fermenta and Zhejiang Garden.
- **Active US DMFs:** DSM, Fermenta, Dishman Netherlands, Taizhou Hisound and BASF (formulated form). NHU and Kingdomway hold no cholecalciferol DMF or CEP, and Garden's US DMF is inactive.

Analogue APIs are a different, specialist field. The repeat holders across calcitriol, alfacalcidol, calcipotriol, calcifediol, paricalcitol and doxercalciferol are Formosa Laboratories (Taiwan), TAPI NL (Teva), Cerbios (CH), Carbogen Amcis/Dishman (NL/India) and Chinese entrants (ALP Pharm Beijing, Chongqing Huapont, Nanjing Heron, Anhui Haoyuan). Among Indian firms, Sun Pharma (doxercalciferol), Neuland and Sai Life (paricalcitol) appear. Symbiotec holds about 45 active US DMFs, all corticosteroids and sex hormones, and none for any vitamin D compound. The analogue markets are small in tonnage (kg-scale) and valued in the tens to low hundreds of USD millions per molecule.

### Inferences
- **Calcifediol is where D3 makers are moving.** Garden already has tens of tonnes of feed-grade 25-OH-D3 crystal capacity, Fermenta is validating pharma calcifediol, and only Carbogen Amcis, Formosa and Łukasiewicz hold valid calcifediol CEPs. Faes (about €63.5m) and OPKO (about USD 30m) show a therapeutic calcifediol market of roughly USD 100m+ in finished-dose value. Pharma API volumes are kg-scale (daily doses of µg).
- **Low tonnage, few qualified suppliers.** At 0.25 to 1 µg daily doses, global analogue API need is in kilograms. For example, 1 kg of calcitriol = 4 billion 0.25 µg capsules. The markets are small in API revenue but have meaningful regulatory barriers. Each molecule has about 5 to 10 qualified CEP/DMF holders, with Formosa, TAPI, Cerbios and Carbogen Amcis/Dishman present in nearly all, and Chinese filers (Huapont, Heron, Haoyuan, ALP) adding capacity since 2013. Price competition is likely intensifying.
- **Implication for Symbiotec.** It has no vitamin D DMFs or CEPs, so any vitamin D3 or analogue business would be a greenfield regulatory build. Its steroid chemistry may be relevant to cholesterol/7-DHC-derived secosteroids (see Gaps on chemistry), but incumbents are entrenched.
- **Dishman Carbogen Amcis is the Indian group with the broadest vitamin D footprint.** Through its Dutch entities (Dishman Netherlands B.V. and Carbogen Amcis B.V., Veenendaal) it covers cholecalciferol, oily D3, calcitriol, alfacalcidol, calcifediol, paricalcitol and cholesterol HP. It also has NF cholesterol capacity (about 250 t per Pacific Securities). Its cholecalciferol CEP was withdrawn in 2023 and an Indian-entity D3 DMF (34148) is inactive, which suggests de-emphasis of bulk D3 in favour of analogues.

### Gaps
- **Synthesis routes.** No source retrieved here describes the commercial routes for calcitriol, paricalcitol, calcipotriol or eldecalcitol, for example whether they are convergent syntheses from vitamin D2/D3-derived CD-ring synthons plus A-ring phosphine oxides, or built from cholesterol or other steroid feedstocks. So whether Symbiotec's steroid synthons (phytosterol-derived intermediates) are transferable is **not verified**. A process-chemistry review or patent search is needed.
- No current (2024-26) IQVIA US sales for generic paricalcitol, calcitriol, doxercalciferol or calcipotriene were found. Chugai's Edirol and Oxarol 2025 sales and LEO's Daivobet/Enstilar sales were not broken out.
- Indian DMFs for vitamin D analogues (CDSCO) and Japanese DMFs (e.g., Microbiopharm Japan, Chugai eldecalcitol API sourcing) were not checked.
- Teva's TAPI divestment status and how it affects the TAPI NL vitamin D site were not checked.

## 5. Value pricing by grade (feed vs food/supplement vs USP/EP pharma vs crystalline)

### Takeaway
Feed-grade D3 500 is a highly cyclical commodity:
- Chinese prices have swung between about RMB 50 and 625/kg over 2014-24.
- They were at RMB 220 to 250/kg in early 2025, then fell sharply in 2026 (Fermenta animal realisations -40% YoY in Q1 FY27).

Food, supplement and pharma grades are described as "relatively stable". Mid-2025 food/supplement-grade D3 100,000 IU/g quotes (about USD 13.2/kg) imply roughly 2x the per-IU price of feed D3 500 at the same time. No public price was found for USP/EP crystalline (40 MIU/g).

### Inferences
- **Per-IU (pure-D3-equivalent) price ladder**, my conversions:
  - Feed D3 500 at RMB 240/kg (about USD 33/kg) = 12.5 g pure per kg, or about USD 2,600 to 2,700 per kg of pure D3.
  - At the 2023 trough (RMB 60/kg) it was about USD 660/kg pure.
  - Food/supplement D3 100 at USD 13.2/kg = 2.5 g pure per kg, or about USD 5,300 per kg of pure D3 (mid-2025). That is about 2x feed at peak and about 8x feed at trough.
  - USP/EP crystal and CEP-backed pharma forms likely sit above the beadlet price per IU, given the regulatory premium, but this is not confirmed by any price source.
- **Value share swings with the feed cycle.** Because feed prices swing 3 to 9x within cycles while human-grade prices are stable, the feed share of total D3 value does too. It is low in trough years (2019-20, 2022-23 and probably 2026) and high in spike years (2017-18, H2 2024 to H1 2025).

## 6. Growth drivers: post-COVID normalisation, deficiency, fortification, feed growth, 25-OH-D3

### Takeaway
Human-nutrition D3 demand has normalised after a 2020-21 COVID spike and destocking:
- Fermenta's human D3 grew +62% in FY25 and +29% in FY26 on "normalisation of post-pandemic customer inventory".
- US NBJ data showed vitamin D falling post-pandemic but less than vitamin C.

Structural drivers:
- Deficiency prevalence, e.g., 1 in 5 Indians deficient (ICRIER).
- Ageing.
- Plant-source/vegan D3: India's FSSAI allows only plant-source vitamin D in fortified foods, and Fermenta got FSSAI approval for VITADEE Green in Jul 2026.
- Steady feed growth: global feed about 1.29 bn t, D3 demand growth about 4 to 6% a year.
- 25-OH-D3 (calcifediol) partly replacing D3 in feed.

### Inferences
- **Feed volume** should grow roughly with global compound-feed output (low single digits). Feed value will be driven far more by Chinese supply discipline and the DSM/CVC restructuring than by demand. 2026 appears to be a down-leg after the 2024-25 spike, based on Fermenta's -40% realisation and Garden's margin compression.
- **Human nutrition growth** is now organic again (mid-single to low-double digits) after the 2022-23 destocking.
- **India** could become a structurally larger D3 consumer if FSSAI makes fortification mandatory. Even then, tonnage is modest: fortifying edible oil at FSSAI-type levels of a few µg per 10 g serving would need only a few tonnes of pure D3 for India's whole edible-oil supply. That is my rough inference; the FSSAI dose was not retrieved.
- The **plant-source-only rule** for vitamin D fortification in India favours vegan D3 (Fermenta VITADEE Green, lichen D3) or D2 over lanolin-derived D3 in that channel.
- **25-OH-D3/calcifediol** is emerging as both a feed premium product (Garden, DSM Hy-D) and a pharma therapeutic (Faes, OPKO, Fermenta's pipeline). That links the D3 commodity chain to higher-value analogue pharma.

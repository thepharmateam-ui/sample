# Vitamin D3 market size: public primary documents (India, Europe, US)

Compiled 21 Oct 2026 for the Symbiotec Pharmalab note. The brief was to find D3 market-size figures in public primary documents and to exclude broker research and paid market-research web summaries. A consultant report reproduced in an RHP counts as public.

Downloaded source files are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-sample/da2696f9-0447-5145-866a-ae62d71abce7/scratchpad/pubsrc/` (one subfolder per source).

**Units caveat for every tonnage below.** D3 is traded in many strengths:
- Pure crystalline D3 is 40 MIU/g.
- Feed-grade "D3 500" is 500,000 IU/g, so 1 t of pure D3 is about 80 t of D3 500.
- Food/pharma forms include 100 CWS, oils and others.

Most "tonnes of D3" figures are on a commercial-product basis, often D3 500, and the source rarely says so. Pure-equivalent conversions marked "my inference" are mine, not the source's.

## A. Best citable figures (ranked by usefulness)

### A1. IFEEDER / Decision Innovation Solutions and Lobo Consulting Solutions (US), November 2025: global D3 supply, demand and capacity, 2023

- **Document:** "Strategic Assessment on Impact of Vitamin and Amino Acid Supply Chain Disruptions on U.S. Food Security", November 2025, 183 pp.
  - Prepared by Decision Innovation Solutions LLC (DIS) and Lobo Consulting Solutions LLC (LCS) for the Institute for Feed Education and Research (IFEEDER), the non-profit research arm of the American Feed Industry Association. It was funded by IFEEDER.
  - PDF metadata creation date: 12 Nov 2025.
  - It is freely downloadable and is not a paid market-research product.
- **URLs:**
  - Landing page: https://www.ifeeder.org/research/analyzing-the-vitamin-and-amino-acid-supply-chain/
  - Full report: https://www.afia.org/pub/?id=c9faec10-dada-d077-7081-0e9bd8e1bcee
  - Summary: https://www.afia.org/pub/?id=e4427f96-c128-94cd-4bc5-7ee294c88826
- **Figures:**
  - **Global D3 supply 2023: 3,640 MT. Global D3 demand 2023: about 3,755 MT. Global D3 production capacity 2023: about 19,000 MT, flat to June 2025.** Capacity utilisation was 20%, the lowest of all vitamins and amino acids studied. China held about 93% of capacity and the rest of the world 7%. Source: printed p.23 (PDF p.32), section 3.3.3.
    - Quote: "Decision Innovation Solutions and Lobo Consulting Solutions estimate 2023 global vitamin D3 production capacity at about 19,000 MT. Multiple industry sources have corroborated the estimate."
    - Quote: "Decision Innovation Solutions and Lobo Consulting Solutions estimate global vitamin D3 supply for 2023 at 3,640 MT and global demand at approximately 3,755 MT. Vitamin D3 global production capacity utilization for 2023 was 20%, the lowest of all vitamins and amino acids studied."
    - Quote: "…China represented approximately 93% of global production capacity in 2023 and ROW constituted 7%… an industry source has indicated that the global vitamin D3 industry relies on a precursor made exclusively in China."
  - D3 is 6.1% of the roughly 301,000 MT global capacity for the eight vitamins studied, which implies about 18,400 MT for D3. Source: printed p.15 (PDF p.24).
  - **US animal-feed supplemental vitamin D: 86 MT (2023).** Source: exec-summary table, printed p.2 (PDF p.11), "Data for 2023. Source: US comtrade, Various Industry Sources, DIS Calculations".
  - **85% of US vitamin D imports came from China (2024, Comtrade).** Source: printed p.3 (PDF p.12). Quote: "…92% of thiamine (B1), 85% of vitamin D, 68% of vitamin E… imported into the U.S. originated from China."
- **Underlying source:** DIS/LCS own estimates, "corroborated" by unnamed industry sources, plus UN Comtrade. No broker or paid market-research publisher is cited.
- **Caveats:**
  1. The p.2 table puts "3,639" under the heading "Global Production Capacity" for Vitamin D (D3). That conflicts with the text on p.23 (capacity 19,000 MT; supply 3,640 MT). The table cell is almost certainly the 2023 supply figure. Cite the p.23 text.
  2. The tonnage basis is not stated. 3,755 MT as pure D3 would be about 150 quadrillion IU, which is implausible against global feed needs. On a D3 500 basis it is about 1.9 × 10^15 IU, or about 47 t of pure D3, which is plausible (my inference). Treat it as commercial-product tonnes.
  3. The figures are well below Chinese broker estimates: capacity about 14,600–14,740 t and demand about 6,750–10,836 t, both on a D3 500 basis (see `Vitamin D3 supply landscape/demand_customers_segments.md`). IFEEDER's capacity is higher and its demand lower.

### A2. European Commission Decision 2003/2/EC (Vitamins cartel, Case COMP/E-1/37.512), adopted 21 Nov 2001: EEA and world D3 market, 1994–98

- **Document:** Commission Decision of 21 November 2001 relating to a proceeding pursuant to Article 81 EC and Article 53 EEA (Case COMP/E-1/37.512 – Vitamins), notified under C(2001) 3695.
  - Published in OJ L 6, 10.1.2003, pp. 1–89.
  - Issuer: European Commission (DG Competition).
- **URLs:**
  - https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2003/2(1)/oj/eng (CELEX 32003D0002)
  - Full OJ PDF via the Publications Office: http://publications.europa.eu/resource/celex/32003D0002
- **Figures:**
  - **EEA market 1998: about ECU 20 million.** Source: recital 60, p. L 6/7. Quote: "The European market for vitamin D3 in 1998 was worth some ECU 20 million."
  - **Community/EEA D3 market value, 1994–1998 (ECU million): 16, 19, 20, 17, 20.** Source: recital 18 table, p. L 6/4 ("Source: Roche market share statistics"). The same table puts all vitamins in scope at ECU 784m in 1998.
  - **Worldwide D3 turnover by producer, 1997 (ECU million, with share):** Roche 26 (40%), Solvay 21 (32%), BASF 11 (15%), Rhône-Poulenc 6 (9%), others 3 (4%). Implied world total is about **ECU 67 million** (my sum). EEA-wide: 6/9/4/2/1, about ECU 22m (my sum). Source: section 1.2.6 table "Vitamin D3 – Turnover (1997, ECU million) and market share (1994 to 1997)", p. L 6/13. The EEA sum of 22 differs slightly from the recital 18 figure of 17 for 1997.
  - **World feed-grade D3 volume, in "TU":**
    - 1994 estimate: 1,450 TU (Solvay 600, Roche 550, BASF 300). Recital 463, p. L 6/46.
    - 1995 estimate: 1,490 TU. Recital 474, p. L 6/47.
    - 1996 estimate: 1,600 TU. Recital 476.
    - 1996 realised: 1,541 TU. Recital 477.
    - Quote: "For 1994 they estimated the world market for vitamin D3 (feed grade) at some 1 450 TU…"
    - "TU" is not defined. Recital 465 calls the same 1996 base "1 600 tonnes". If TU means 10^12 IU, 1,541 TU is about 38.5 t of pure D3 (my inference).
  - **Mix:** "Only about 10 % of the vitamin D3 market (by value) is accounted for by human food applications: the vast bulk of production is used in animal feed." Recital 56, p. L 6/7.
  - **Supplier context:** Solvay (ex-Duphar) "produced about half the world requirement of vitamin D3 in the early 1990s". Recital 58, p. L 6/7.
- **Underlying source:** the cartel's own documents (Solvay's leniency submissions) and Roche market statistics. This is a primary regulatory finding.
- **Caveat:** the data are 25–30 years old. Use it as a historical, citable anchor, not as a current size.

## C. How the figures fit together (my analysis, not from sources)

- **Volume:** the citable non-broker volume anchor is IFEEDER/DIS-LCS.
  - 2023 global D3 demand is about 3,755 MT and supply 3,640 MT, apparently on a commercial-product basis.
  - Capacity is about 19,000 MT, so utilisation is about 20%. China holds 93% of capacity per IFEEDER and 83% per FEFAC.
  - FEFAC's "capacity 5x needs" and IFEEDER's "20% utilisation" independently support a heavily overbuilt, China-dominated market.
- **Value:** no current public primary document gives a D3 value in USD or EUR.
  - The only primary value is the EU cartel decision: EEA about ECU 20m (1998) and world about ECU 67m in producer turnover (1997). About 90% of that value was feed.
  - All current "USD 1–2.3bn" global figures in Fermenta documents trace to MarketsandMarkets or similar paid market research.
- **India:**
  - Trade: DGCI&S HS 29362940 shows India imported US$14.4m and exported US$27.8m of vitamin D in FY26.
  - Retail: the domestic D3 formulation market was ₹5.38bn (MAT Jul 2018), per EY/CII as reproduced in the DIL/Fermenta FY19 AR.
  - No public document gives India D3 API consumption in tonnes.

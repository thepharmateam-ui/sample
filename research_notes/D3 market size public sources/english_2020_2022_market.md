# Vitamin D3 market size, 2020 and 2022: English-language sources

Compiled 21 Oct 2026 for the Symbiotec Pharmalab note. The goal was English sources giving the *actual* global D3 market for 2020 and 2022 (or the nearest years, 2019–2023), in tonnes and/or USD/EUR, to serve as bases for a 2026 extrapolation. Public documents were preferred; broker and paid market research are kept in separate, labelled tiers.

New downloads are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-sample/da2696f9-0447-5145-866a-ae62d71abce7/scratchpad/en2022/`:
- `fermtr/`: Fermenta concall transcripts from BSE
- `crn/`, `shic/`, `acad/`, `mr/`, `cn/` (NHU English annual reports), `img/` (rendered chart pages)

Earlier downloads used here: `.../scratchpad/pubsrc/ifeeder/`, `.../pubsrc/fermbse/`, `.../scratchpad/bp/`.

**Unit convention.** Pure crystalline D3 is 40 MIU/g, and feed-grade "D3 500" is 500,000 IU/g, so 1 t pure = 80 t D3 500. Pure-equivalent conversions marked "(mine)" are my arithmetic, not the source's.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **No English public primary document I found states the global D3 market for calendar 2020 or 2022 as a single number.** The 2020 actual-output figure (6,250 t, D3 500 basis, Boyar) exists only in Chinese, in Garden Bio's CB prospectus (see `china_public_filings.md`).
2. **Best English volume datapoints, nearest to each year:**

| Year | Figure | Basis | What it measures | Source (type) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ~2019, pre-COVID (said Nov 2021 and Feb 2022) | **10,000–12,000 MT**, "dropped" during the pandemic | Unstated; D3 500 implied (see A1) | Global (animal-feed) market volume, management estimate | Fermenta Biotech earnings-call transcripts filed with BSE (company filing) |
| 2019 (published Mar 2021) | Demand **~6,750 t**, capacity **~14,600 t**, China output **5,650 t** | "equivalent to 500,000 IU" | Demand / capacity / output | BP Equities Fermenta initiation (**broker**; cites Boyar). Fallback only |
| 2020 (Aug 2020 article) | Garden ">3,000 tons", ">40% of the industry", so the industry is **< ~7,500 t** (mine) | Unstated | Producer output share | ECHEMI, citing China Feed Industry Information Network (trade press / aggregator) |
| 2023 | Supply **3,640 MT**, demand **~3,755 MT**, capacity **~19,000 MT** | **Unstated**; inconsistent with any single basis (see A3) | Actual supply and demand, plus capacity | IFEEDER/AFIA, Nov 2025 (institutional) |
| Undated, current | **~100 t/yr** worldwide production | Pure D3 (≈ 8,000 t D3 500, mine) | Production | Schütz et al. (dsm-firmenich), *Org. Process Res. Dev.* 2024 (academic, producer-authored) |

3. **IFEEDER check (as briefed).** IFEEDER gives **2023 only**: no 2020 or 2022 series, and no forecast because of a "significant lack of available data". It never states the tonnage basis.
4. **Value.** No English public primary document gives a global D3 value for 2020 or 2022. Two things are available instead:
   - The only value anchors are paid market research on the *vitamin D* market: MarketsandMarkets **USD 1.1bn (2020)** and **USD 1.3bn (2022)**.
   - Fermenta's BSE decks reproduce Feedinfo feed-grade D3 500 price ranges: **2020: EUR 9–60/kg; 2022: EUR 7–16/kg**. These allow a volume × price cross-check (section C).
5. **Main reconciliation issue.** Fermenta's 10–12 kt matches Chinese *demand-type* estimates: CITIC 10,308 t (2021) and Tianfeng 10,836 t (theoretical). It is about 1.6–1.9x Boyar's 2020 *output* of 6,250 t and about 3x IFEEDER's 2023 supply. The sources disagree because they measure different things (demand vs. output) on different (often unstated) bases, not because the market moved that much.

- **1 Jun 2022 (Q4 FY22):** https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/da18ed16-88b0-4526-9427-52389fb047b1.pdf
  - PDF p.8: "Our market share in animal feed is closer to 4% to 5%."
  - PDF p.10: "close to 25% global market share on the human side while we have less than 5% on the animal side."
  - Also: "Volumes we never give…"

**Assessment**
- **Year:** "before pandemic", so about 2019 and possibly 2018–19 average. Volume in 2020–21 was lower, but no number is given. The figure was stated in Nov 2021 and repeated in Feb 2022.
- **Scope:** ambiguous. The Nov 2021 call calls it the "animal feed market"; the Feb 2022 call calls it "global volumes".
- **Basis:** not stated. D3 500 is implied (my inference) for three reasons:
  1. The price references ("$10 or $20 or $30"; "6 dollar and 30 dollar") are D3 500 feed prices. Fermenta's decks chart the same product in EUR/kg.
  2. On a pure basis, 10–12 kt would be absurd (about 100x world output).
  3. Fermenta's 5–6% share would be about 500–720 t D3 500-equivalent a year, plausible for its animal sales.
- **Underlying source:** management estimate, unsourced. The previous Fermenta transcripts listed on screener.in were checked; Fermenta has filed no transcripts after Jun 2022 (decks only).
- **Pure equivalent:** about 125–150 t (mine).
- **Source type:** company filing (stock-exchange-filed transcript). This is the only English company document found with a global D3 tonnage.

- **Prepared by:** Decision Innovation Solutions (DIS) and Lobo Consulting Solutions (LCS) for IFEEDER.
- **URLs:**
  - https://www.afia.org/pub/?id=c9faec10-dada-d077-7081-0e9bd8e1bcee
  - Public mirror, file dated 251107: https://www.pig333.com/3tres3_common/art/pig333/21943/fitxers/251107_FINAL_IFEEDER_Vitamin_&_Amino_Acid_Report_IFEEDER_(PUBLIC).pdf
- **Local copy:** `pubsrc/ifeeder/full.pdf`.

**Figures (printed p.23, PDF p.32, §3.3.3)**
> "…estimate 2023 global vitamin D3 production capacity at about 19,000 MT."

> "…estimate global vitamin D3 supply for 2023 at 3,640 MT and global demand at approximately 3,755 MT. Vitamin D3 global production capacity utilization for 2023 was 20%…"

> "Due to a significant lack of available data, DIS/LCS was unable to develop reasonable vitamin D3 supply and demand forecasts for 2025. DIS/LCS estimates that global production capacity remained flat at 19,000 MT from 2023 to June 2025."

**What I checked**
- **No 2020 or 2022 figures anywhere in the report.**
  - Figure 6 (printed p.16, PDF p.25; rendered image at `en2022/img/if25-025.png`) is a 2023 bar chart: D3 capacity about 19 kt against supply and demand of about 3.6–3.8 kt, "Source: Multiple Industry Sources".
  - The executive-summary table (printed p.2) is headed "Data for 2023". It puts "3,639" in the "Global Production Capacity" column for D3, which contradicts p.23; the cell is evidently the supply figure.
  - The only multi-year material is US import trade data (Comtrade 2020–2024, aggregated HS 293629).
- **No tonnage basis stated** for any vitamin.

**Basis problem (my analysis)**
- On a **D3 500** basis, 3,755 MT is only about **47 t pure**. That is roughly half the about 100 t pure cited by dsm-firmenich authors (A4), and well below Boyar's 2020 output of 6,250 t D3 500 (about 78 t pure).
- On a **1 MIU/g** basis, demand would be about 94 t pure, which fits A4. But capacity would then be about 475 t pure, about 2.6x the Chinese capacity estimates of 14,600–17,300 t D3 500 (about 183–216 t pure).
- No single basis reconciles all three IFEEDER numbers with Chinese data.
- The p.2 table also gives "U.S. Animal Feed Supplement 86 MT" of vitamin D, which looks low on any basis.
- **Treat IFEEDER's 2023 supply and demand as low-confidence.** Its capacity and utilisation direction (heavily overbuilt, about 93% China) is consistent with FEFAC.

**Underlying source:** DIS/LCS estimates "corroborated" by unnamed industry sources. **Source type:** institutional (industry research foundation), public.

## C. Cross-checks and reconciliation (my analysis)

### C1. Volume (D3 500-equivalent unless stated)

| Estimate | t D3 500 | t pure | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boyar 2020 global output (Chinese CB prospectus) | 6,250 | 78 | Actual output |
| BP Equities 2019 global demand (Boyar) | 6,750 | 84 | Demand (broker) |
| Alltech 2020 feed × 6 g/t | 7,126 | 89 | Theoretical feed need |
| Garden CB 2021 feed × 6 g/t | 7,413 | 93 | Theoretical feed need |
| Alltech 2022 feed × 6 g/t | 7,596 | 95 | Theoretical feed need |
| dsm-firmenich authors (OPRD 2024), undated | ≈8,000 | ~100 | Production |
| Fermenta "before pandemic" (said 2021–22) | 10,000–12,000 | 125–150 | Management estimate of market |
| CITIC via Gongtong filing, 2021 use | 10,308 | 129 | Broker demand estimate |
| IFEEDER 2023 supply / demand | 3,640 / 3,755 | 45 / 47 if D3 500 | Consultant estimate, basis unstated |

**Reading the table**
- **Actual output in 2020** was about 6,000–6,500 t D3 500 (about 80 t pure). Boyar is the only actual-output figure; the ECHEMI share arithmetic and the pure-basis ~100 t are consistent with it.
- **Demand-style estimates** for 2020–22 run about 7,100–10,300 t: about 7,100–7,600 t for feed at 6 g/t, and about 10,000+ t once human use and higher inclusion rates are added.
- **No English or Chinese public source gives 2022 actual output.** For a 2022 base, rolling 2020 output forward with feed growth (+6.6% from 2020 to 2022 on Alltech) gives about **6,650 t** (mine). The feed-requirement method gives about **7,600 t**.
- **IFEEDER's 2023 numbers sit well below everything else** unless they are on a different basis. Do not splice IFEEDER onto Boyar or Fermenta as one time series.
- **Fermenta vs Boyar:** the 10–12 kt is a management "market size" quoted for *pre-pandemic* conditions. It is closer to broker demand models than to Boyar output, and it includes Chinese stock-building. Use it as an upper bound.

### C2. Value from volume × price (mine)

Prices are Feedinfo D3 500 ranges as shown in Fermenta's decks.
- **2020:** 6,250 t × EUR 9–60/kg ≈ **EUR 56–375m**. 2020 was a spike year (China up 343% Jan–Apr 2020; peak EUR 60/kg), so the annual average sits nearer the low-to-middle of the range.
- **2022:** 7,596 t × EUR 7–16/kg ≈ **EUR 53–122m**. On IFEEDER-type volumes (3,640 t) it would be EUR 25–58m.
- **What this covers:** about 80% of volume (feed) only. Human-grade D3 (crystal, oils, CWS) is about 20% of volume but carries a far higher price per IU. No public source prices or sizes it in value.
- **Comparison with market research:** a bulk-ingredient D3 market of the order of USD 0.1–0.4bn is plausible. That is consistent with the lower D3-only publisher figures (USD 180m in 2021; USD 337.5m in 2024) and well below the USD 1.1–1.6bn "vitamin D" totals, which evidently include D2, analogues and/or downstream product value.
- **For extrapolation:** 2020 is a poor value base because of the price spike, and 2022 is a price trough. Extrapolate volume and price separately.

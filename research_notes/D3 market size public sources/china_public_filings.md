# Vitamin D3 market size in Chinese public primary documents (non-broker)

Compiled 2026-10-21 for the Symbiotec Pharmalab note. Scope: exchange filings (prospectuses, offering documents, replies to exchange/CSRC inquiries, legal opinions, sponsor letters, feasibility reports, annual reports), regulator decisions and official trade/association statistics from China. Broker research and paid market-research publishers were excluded as sources. Where a public filing itself relays a broker or consultant figure, this is flagged.

"PDF p." is the page number in the PDF file. "Printed p." is the page number printed on the page (the 1-1-xxx style used in Chinese prospectuses).

## 0. Unit convention (read first)

Nearly all Chinese filings state D3 tonnage **"折 50 万 I.U./g"**, i.e. in feed-grade powder equivalent at 500,000 IU/g. Pure crystalline D3 is 40 million IU/g (Garden AR glossary: "维生素 D3 结晶 指 含 4000 万 I.U./g 的纯品维生素 D3 产品"; 1 μg = 40 IU). So **1 t of pure D3 = 80 t at 500 kIU/g**.

| Figure (500 kIU/g basis) | Pure-D3 equivalent |
|---|---|
| 14,793 t (Garden 2023 CB, theoretical demand) | ~185 t |
| 14,600 t (2020 global capacity, Boyar via Garden 2023 CB) | ~183 t |
| 6,250 t (2020 global output, Boyar via Garden 2023 CB) | ~78 t |
| 7,413 t (2021 global feed-grade demand, Garden 2023 CB) | ~93 t |
| 7,250 t (Garden 2014 IPO, global demand) | ~91 t |

**How to use them.** Figure #2 (6,250 t of output in 2020) is the best number for actual market volume. Figure #1 (14,793 t) is a theoretical "addressable demand" built on population × recommended dose. It is about 2.4x actual output and should be labelled theoretical. Figure #1's feed sub-component (7,413 t) is the most defensible demand-side number because it is feed output × inclusion rate. Both #1 and #2 appear in a final, CSRC-registered prospectus.

**No primary Chinese public document found gives a D3 market *value* (USD/RMB).** The nearest is Kingdomway's 2015 feasibility report: D3 ≈ 2% of global vitamin market value, from Boyar (see 3.7).

Earlier drafts were the 申报稿 of 2022-08-12 (http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2022-08-12/1214284315.PDF) and the 修订稿 of 2022-10-28/2022-11-02.
- Section: 第四节/六、发行人所处行业基本情况 → "4、维生素 D3 市场需求状况"

**(a) Feed demand.** PDF p.102–103, printed 1-1-101 to 1-1-102.
> 根据奥特奇（Alltech）发布的《Agri-Food Outlook 2022》，全球饲料产量在过去一年中增长 2.3%，2021 年估计产量为 12.355 亿吨，中国的饲料产量为 2.614 亿吨，是最大的饲料生产国。按照每吨饲料中维生素 D3 添加量为 6g 估算，全球饲料级维生素 D3 需求量约为 7,413 吨，其中中国饲料级维生素 D3 需求量约为 1,568 吨。

EN: "According to Alltech's *Agri-Food Outlook 2022*, global feed output grew 2.3% in the past year, with 2021 output estimated at 1,235.5 Mt. Assuming 6 g of vitamin D3 per tonne of feed, global feed-grade vitamin D3 demand is about 7,413 t, of which China accounts for about 1,568 t."

**(b) Food fortification.** PDF p.103, printed 1-1-102.
> 截止 2022 年 5 月，世界人口为 78.98 亿人，中国人口为 14.74 亿人。按照每人平均每天补充 400I.U.维生素 D3 估算，全球营养强化食品每年对维生素 D3 的理论需求量约为 2,306 吨（含量：50 万 I.U./g），其中中国营养强化食品对维生素 D3 的理论需求量约为 430 吨（含量：50 万 I.U./g）。

EN: "As of May 2022 world population was 7.898 bn and China's 1.474 bn. At an average supplementation of 400 IU of D3 per person per day, theoretical annual D3 demand from fortified food is about 2,306 t globally (500 kIU/g), of which China is about 430 t."

**(c) Pharma/health.** PDF p.103–104, printed 1-1-102 to 1-1-103. Assumes 40% of the population is deficient and takes 2,200 IU/day. Dose sources cited: the literature review 《维生素 D 缺乏相关性疾病研究进展》 and a China CDC nutrition institute report.
> 据此估算，全球医药保健行业对维生素 D3 的理论需求量约为 5,074 吨（含量：50 万 I.U./g），其中中国医药保健行业对维生素 D3 的理论需求量约为 930 吨（含量：50 万 I.U./g）。

EN: "On this basis, theoretical D3 demand from the pharma/health sector is about 5,074 t globally (500 kIU/g), of which China is about 930 t."

**(d) Total.** PDF p.104, printed 1-1-103.
> 综上所述，全球范围内对维生素 D3 的理论需求约为 14,793 吨，其中我国对维生素 D3 的理论需求约为 2,928 吨。

EN: "In sum, global theoretical demand for vitamin D3 is about 14,793 t, of which China's is about 2,928 t."

- Original data source: the company's own arithmetic. The feed input is Alltech; the population inputs are UN/national figures. **No broker is cited.** My checks: 12.355 bn t × 6 g = 7,413 t, and 78.98 bn × 0.4 × 2,200 IU × 365 / 500,000 IU/g = 5,074 t. The arithmetic reconciles.
- Caveat: the food and pharma lines are theoretical "need" calculations, not consumption. Note also an internal inconsistency in the source: China's population is 14.74 亿 in (b) but 14.47 亿 in (c).

**(e) Capacity and output table.** PDF p.376, printed 1-1-375, section "3、关于公司扩产维生素产品所占市场份额的说明".

Competitors listed: 帝斯曼、印度迪氏曼、花园生物、金达威、新和成、浙江医药、台州海盛、威仕生物、山东同辉、天新药业.

## 5. Official statistics: customs and associations

- **China Customs HS code:** D3 has **no separate 8-digit code** in China's current tariff. It falls in 2936.2900 / 2936290000 "其他未混合的维生素及其衍生物" (other unmixed vitamins and their derivatives). That code also covers biotin, vitamin K and other items, so official export statistics cannot isolate D3.
  - A 10-digit sub-code **2936290010 "胆钙化醇"** (cholecalciferol) existed, with pesticide-registration control "S", but is marked obsolete in 2022 (per hsciq.com: https://hsciq.com/en/HSCN/Code/2936290010).
  - Current 2936290000 CIQ sub-codes (101 feed additive; 301–304) do not name D3 (https://hsciq.com/en/HSCN/Code/2936290000).
  - GACC's public query platform (stats.customs.gov.cn) publishes at 8-digit level. **No official D3 export series is available.** Not verified on the GACC site itself.
- **China Feed Industry Association** (全国饲料工业发展概况): publishes total feed-additive and vitamin output only. No D3 line found.
- **China Chemical Pharmaceutical Industry Association** and **CCCMHPIE (医保商会):** no published D3-specific volume or value found.

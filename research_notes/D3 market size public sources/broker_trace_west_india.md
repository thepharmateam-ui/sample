## Claim 1: dsm-firmenich D3 capacity closure

**Broker wording** (Dongwu/Soochow Securities, NHU initiation, 16 Jul 2025, PDF p.17 of 36; local `scratchpad/nhu/dw.pdf`):
> "部分海外企业由于能源价格高企，面临中国企业的竞争，选择退出市场。以帝斯曼为例，2023-2024 年，帝斯曼陆续关停 VC、VB、VD3 产能。2024 年 2 月，帝斯曼宣布剥离动物营养板块…"

Translation: some overseas producers exited because of high energy prices and Chinese competition; DSM progressively shut VC, VB and VD3 capacity in 2023–24. No source is cited.

### What dsm-firmenich itself said: nothing about closing D3

I checked every primary below. None mentions closing, exiting or impairing vitamin D3 production.

   > "Restructuring of the vitamin asset footprint, significantly reducing costs. This includes the closure of the Xinghuo vitamin B6 plant in China and the refocusing of the company's vitamin C activities on its specialty Quali®-C from Dalry (UK) only. ### Verdict and quotable alternative

**No public dsm-firmenich primary supports "DSM shut D3 capacity in 2023–24".** The VC and VB parts of the broker line are supported (Jiangshan vitamin C; Xinghuo B6). The D3 part is not, and dsm-firmenich's website, EDQM records and brochure point the other way.

Suggested wording:
> "dsm-firmenich's 2023 vitamin restructuring closed its Xinghuo (China) vitamin B6 plant and shut vitamin C output at Jiangshan (China), with extended vitamin A/E shutdowns at Sisseln (press release, 28 Jun 2023; IAR 2023). The company has not announced any vitamin D3 closure: it still describes itself as 'the world's largest manufacturer of vitamin D3', produced at Sisseln, and holds valid EDQM CEPs for cholecalciferol, one renewed in 2025 and one revised in Feb 2026. It buys cholesterol, 7-DHC and 25-OH-D3 from Zhejiang Garden under long-term agreements (Garden IR, Apr 2025), and Garden said in Apr 2026 that its contract with DSM had been renewed. DSM's vitamins business, D3 included, moves to the CVC-controlled 'Essential Products Company' on closing, expected end-2026 (dsm-firmenich, 9 Feb 2026)."

If the analyst wants to describe the market rumour, attribute it generically ("Chinese industry commentary suggests…") and do not present it as fact.

**Matches, with a date caveat.** Both figures are Fermenta management's own 2021 estimates. On 1 Jul 2021 management described the 25% as applying "until last year", that is, pre-pandemic expansion. No newer percentage has been published.

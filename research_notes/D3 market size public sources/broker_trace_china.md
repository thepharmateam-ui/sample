# Tracing Chinese-broker vitamin D3 figures to quotable public primaries

Compiled 2026-10-21 for the Symbiotec Pharmalab note. The aim is to replace figures taken from Tianfeng Securities (16 Dec 2024), Pacific Securities (29 Jul 2024) and BP Equities (Mar 2021) with documents the user can cite directly. Those are company filings, exchange documents, regulator decisions, statutory disclosures, and publicly readable data-vendor pages (the vendor pages are flagged).

Builds on `china_public_filings.md` (same folder) and `../Vitamin D3 supply landscape/conventional_lanolin_d3_supply.md`. Those notes are not repeated here.

## 4. Tianfeng theoretical demand of 10,836 t

**Broker text** (`tf/tf.txt` lines 570–575): "根据奥特奇发布的《农业食品展望 2024》报告，2023 年全球饲料产量达到 12.9 亿吨，从中国饲料工业协会网的数据来看，2023 年全国工业饲料总产量达到 3.22 亿吨。根据德国巴斯夫公司及瑞士罗氏公司对维生素 D3 在各类动物全价配合饲料中的添加量（2022 版）以及国标（GB 9840-2017）饲料添加剂的要求，按照每吨饲料约添加 8.4 克维生素 D3，全球大约需要 10836 吨维生素 D3，仅中国就需要约 2704.8 吨维生素 D3。"

EN: "Per Alltech's *Agri-Food Outlook 2024*, 2023 global feed output reached 1.29 bn t; per the China Feed Industry Association, China's 2023 industrial feed output was 322 Mt. Based on BASF's and Roche's recommended D3 levels in complete feeds (2022 edition) and the national standard GB 9840-2017, about 8.4 g of D3 per tonne of feed implies global need of about 10,836 t, of which China alone needs about 2,704.8 t."

- **The calculation is Tianfeng's own.**
  - Global: 1.29 bn t × 8.4 g/t = 10.836 bn g = **10,836 t**.
  - China: 322 Mt × 8.4 g/t = **2,704.8 t**. It uses CFIA's figure, not Alltech's China number of 262.71 Mt.
- **Alltech year:** *Agri-Food Outlook 2024*, published early 2024, covering 2023 feed output of **about 1.29 bn t**. That is roughly flat on 2022; 142 countries, more than 27,000 feed mills. Alltech revised the 2023 data on 15 Apr 2024, but the 1.29 bn total is unchanged. Coverage: https://www.feedstuffs.com/agribusiness-news/2024-alltech-agri-food-outlook-world-feed-production-remains-steady. **Cite Alltech's own press release or report** (alltech.com, Agri-Food Outlook); I did not open the PDF itself.
- **China feed:** CFIA, 《2023年全国饲料工业发展概况》, 321.63 Mt (32,163 万吨) per secondary reports. Cite CFIA directly.
- **The weak link is the 8.4 g/t.**
  - It is Tianfeng's choice, attributed to BASF/Roche recommendations. Roche's vitamin business has been part of DSM since 2003, so the "Roche (2022 edition)" reference is odd. GB 9840-2017 is a product-quality standard, not a dosing rule.
  - 8.4 g of 500 kIU/g product per tonne equals 4,200 IU/kg of feed.
  - To replicate without Tianfeng, choose and cite your own inclusion rate. Options are the DSM OVN vitamin guidelines or BASF recommendations. **Garden's filings use 6 g/t**: Alltech 2023 × 6 g/t = **7,740 t**, which matches the method in Garden's CB prospectus pp.1-1-101–102. BP uses "6–7 g".

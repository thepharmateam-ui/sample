# US Generic Competitive Landscape: Injectable Methylprednisolone (Solu-Medrol MPSS; Depo-Medrol MPA)

Data stamp: compiled 21 Oct 2026. Orange Book (OB) = FDA data file `products.txt` dated 9 Oct 2026 ([FDA OB data files](https://www.fda.gov/media/76860/download)). openFDA Drugs@FDA / NDC / label / shortage endpoints were last updated 20 Oct 2026 ([openFDA Drugs@FDA query](https://api.fda.gov/drug/drugsfda.json?search=products.active_ingredients.name:%22METHYLPREDNISOLONE+SODIUM+SUCCINATE%22&limit=100)). "MPSS" = methylprednisolone sodium succinate for injection. "MPA" = methylprednisolone acetate injectable suspension. "DCV" = dual-chamber vial (Pfizer Act-O-Vial).

## Q1. Who holds approved applications for MPSS injection, when were they approved, in which strengths, and how fast has the field grown (2018–2026)?

### Takeaway
Only 5 companies hold *active* (OB "RX") ANDAs for MPSS: Fresenius Kabi (2004), Sagent (2011), Hikma (2016 and 2022), Amneal (2016) and Tianjin Kingyork (2021). A sixth, Eugia/Aurobindo (2015), is now "Discontinued" in OB. Only **2 MPSS ANDAs were approved in 2018–2026** (Kingyork Apr 2021; Hikma 40/125 mg Sep 2022), and **none since Sep 2022**. So the field is "moderately crowded but static", not growing fast. Most of the familiar names checked (Teva, Mylan/Viatris, Cipla, Zydus, MSN, Slayback, Gland in its own name, Jiangsu filers) hold no MPSS application. The hydrocortisone cross-check supports the user's hypothesis: Solu-Cortef has only 3 ANDAs, all approved 2024–2026 and all 100 mg only.

### Inferences
- The hypothesis "Medrol is crowded, Cortef is not" holds on approval counts. MPSS has 5 active ANDA holders (6 counting Eugia) and up to 7 generic labelers at 40/125 mg (see Q3), with generic entry dating to 2004. HCSS has 3 ANDAs, all approved since Sep 2024 and all limited to 100 mg. The nuance is that MPSS crowding is concentrated at 40/125 mg. The 500 mg, 1 g and especially 2 g strengths are thinner.
- MPSS crowding is *mature, not accelerating*. No ANDA has been approved in 4 years. New "entrants" since 2022 came through distribution deals on existing ANDAs (Dr. Reddy's on Kingyork; Civica on Hikma), not new filings.
- Sagent's ANDA is technically active but appears commercially dormant (no NDC listing). The effective count of active ANDA manufacturers is therefore about 4–5: Fresenius, Hikma, Amneal, Kingyork, plus Eugia if its listings are live.

### Gaps
- OB/Drugs@FDA do not show tentative approvals or pending ANDAs. The number of MPSS ANDAs under review (potential future entrants) is unknown.
- Fresenius Kabi's manufacturing site for MPSS was not captured from the label extract.
- Why Eugia's ANDA moved to DISCN in OB while its NDC listings and SPL remain current (SPL updated Jun 2026) could not be resolved. See Q3.

## Q2. Do any generics replicate the Act-O-Vial dual-chamber vial, or are all generics single-vial lyophilized powder? Is DCV a meaningful differentiator?

### Takeaway
**No US generic MPSS is a DCV.** Every generic label (Fresenius, Hikma, Civica/Hikma, Amneal, Tianjin Kingyork, Dr. Reddy's/Kingyork, Eugia) describes single-chamber vials of lyophilized powder, with no co-packaged diluent or Act-O-Vial-type system. Pfizer is the only DCV source. Its Act-O-Vial is the preservative-free presentation at 40 mg, 125 mg, 500 mg and 1 g. Its 500 mg/1 g multi-dose vials and 2 g vial are conventional vials preserved with benzyl alcohol.

### Inferences
- In 11+ years of generic approvals (2004–2022), no filer has replicated Act-O-Vial. Seven generic labels all chose the single-vial lyophilized format, which points to a real technical, device or regulatory hurdle in the DCV format. The DCV presentation is therefore Pfizer's main remaining point of product differentiation at the high-volume 40/125 mg strengths.
- The heavy concentration of repackagers and relabelers on the brand Act-O-Vial (8 vs 2 for generics) suggests the self-contained DCV is preferred in point-of-use channels: emergency kits, physician offices, sports medicine (Sportpharm) and unit-dose overbags (Cardinal). This is inferred from listing counts only; there is no volume data.
- A US generic DCV, as in the Symbiotec thesis, would be a first-of-its-kind presentation for MPSS. A DCV generic filed as an ANDA against Act-O-Vial would still be AP-rated to the same RLD, but no such application is visible in OB.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence was found on hospital or GPO preference for DCV vs single-vial MPSS, or on any price premium for Act-O-Vial vs generics. IQVIA/Bloomberg pack-level data would answer this.
- It was not verified whether generic 40/125 mg labels specify a particular diluent (Sterile Water vs Bacteriostatic Water). The How Supplied sections list no co-packaged diluent.
- No public information was found on pending DCV ANDAs.

## Q4. Was MPSS (and MPA) in shortage on FDA/ASHP lists in 2017–2026? Dates, causes, resolution?

### Takeaway
MPSS has had **recurring supply disruptions**. ASHP's MPSS shortage page (as cited in a Sep 2025 peer-reviewed paper) showed backorders across Pfizer, Fresenius and Hikma in mid-2025, with Pfizer's 40/125 mg Act-O-Vials on weekly allocation. The openFDA shortage feed shows **no current MPSS entry** as of Oct 2026. MPA (Depo-Medrol and generics) **is in an active FDA-listed shortage**, first posted 15 Dec 2021 and still current at 15 Oct 2026. Causes cited are demand increase, manufacturing delay and, for Sagent, "shortage of an active ingredient". Estimated recovery runs to Dec 2026 to Mar 2027 for some Pfizer NDCs.

### Cited Findings
- **ASHP MPSS shortage (2025):** citing ASHP's "Methylprednisolone Sodium Succinate Injection" page (retrieved 27 Aug 2025), the paper reports that Pfizer had "its 500 mg vials on backorder with an estimated release date of June 2025", and that the 40 mg and 125 mg Act-O-Vial presentations "are available only in limited supply, with allocations released on a weekly basis". Fresenius 40 mg was on backorder (est. late June to early July 2025). Hikma 40 mg, 125 mg and 1 g were backordered (staggered release mid-June to mid-September 2025). The authors state that none of the recalls or current shortages involved Tianjin Kingyork — [Isaacs et al., "NIR Spectrometry Reveals Lack of Content Uniformity in MPSS", PMC12646679, Sep 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12646679/)

### Inferences
- MPSS supply is fragile despite 5+ approved sources. Mid-2025 backorders hit three of the largest suppliers (Pfizer, Fresenius, Hikma) at once. This suggests capacity tightness and some pricing support, especially at 40/125 mg, and the brand DCV was on allocation. Kingyork/Dr. Reddy's and Civica appear to have benefited from these gaps (inference from timing).
- The MPA shortage has lasted almost five years (Dec 2021 to Oct 2026, still "Current"). It spans the brand and most generics, with one generic citing API shortage. That points to a supply-constrained market where a reliable API plus a sterile-suspension maker could take share. This bears on Symbiotec as an MPA API lister.
- The absence of MPSS from the openFDA shortage feed may mean FDA never listed the 2025 episode (ASHP lists more liberally than FDA) or that it was resolved. The data cannot distinguish these.

### Gaps

- The openFDA shortage endpoint appears to carry only current records. FDA's legacy shortage pages for MPSS returned 404, so FDA-listed MPSS shortages in 2017–2024, if any, could not be confirmed.
- Whether MPSS was on FDA/ASHP lists during COVID-19 (2020) could not be confirmed from primary sources.

## Q5. Paragraph IV / CGT / authorized generics / recent entrants, and IQVIA market-size figures quoted in press releases (MPSS)

### Takeaway
There are **no patents or exclusivities** on Solu-Medrol (so no PIV is possible), **no CGT designation** for methylprednisolone, and **no authorized generic** listed. Press releases cite a US MPSS market of about **$102M (IMS, 12 months to Oct 2015)**, about **$123.5M (IMS, 12 months to Jan 2016)** and about **$144M brand+generic (IQVIA retail + non-retail MAT, Feb 2022)**. These suggest the dollar market grew modestly despite generic entry. That may reflect shortage-driven pricing (inference). The latest new entrants are Dr. Reddy's (Apr 2022 launch, on Kingyork's ANDA), Hikma 40/125 mg (Sep 2022) and Civica (Dec 2025, on Hikma's ANDA).

- From about $102–124M (2015–16) to about $144M (Feb 2022), the US MPSS dollar market held up or grew while generic count rose from about 3 to about 6. The figures come from different datasets and scopes (IMS vs IQVIA; the Aurobindo figure may cover only its approved strengths), so the trend is indicative only.
- The lack of any CGT designation and the absence of patents mean MPSS offers no regulatory exclusivity to a new generic entrant. Any DCV advantage would have to come from commercial or technical differentiation, not exclusivity.

### Gaps

## Q1. Which fermentation-API competitors and listed peers does the Concord Biotech RHP (Jul/Aug 2023, F&S) name, and what market shares does it give?

### Takeaway
The Concord RHP's listed peer set is **Divi's, Suven Pharmaceuticals (now Cohance Lifesciences), Laurus Labs and Shilpa Medicare**. It does **not** include Biocon, Gland or Alkem. For global fermentation-API competitors, the F&S section benchmarks Concord against **Biocon, BrightGene, CCSB (Taiwan), CKD Bio (Korea), Zhejiang Hisun, Huadong Medicine and Teva** using DMF counts. It credits Concord with more than 20% volume share in 2022 across its identified fermentation APIs: tacrolimus above 40%, cyclosporine above 30%, mycophenolate sodium about 28%, mupirocin about 22%, mycophenolate mofetil about 15%, everolimus 9% and pimecrolimus 4%.

### Inferences
- The analyst's working assumption that the Concord RHP peers were "Biocon, Divi's, Gland, Laurus, Alkem" is wrong. The actual listed set is **Divi's / Suven / Laurus / Shilpa**. Biocon appears only in the F&S global fermentation-DMF benchmark, not as a valuation peer.
- Concord's global peer set (BrightGene, CCSB, CKD Bio, Hisun, Huadong, Teva, Biocon) is entirely immunosuppressant, oncology and statin fermentation. **None of these companies overlap with Symbiotec's steroid portfolio.** They are relevant to Symbiotec only through its stated pipeline of classical fermentation APIs (mupirocin, immunosuppressants, anti-fungals). Mupirocin directly overlaps with Concord's ~22% share.

### Gaps
- In the PDF text extraction, the coloured tick-marks in the Concord DMF table (US vs Japan vs EU) cannot be told apart, so I could not confirm per-region DMF coverage for each peer.
- The Concord RHP gives no separate sirolimus market share; sirolimus is included only in the "over 20%" aggregate.

### Inferences
- Symbiotec's listed peer set (Concord, Divi's, Cohance, Laurus) contains **no steroid API pure-play**, because none is listed in India. The valuation comparison is effectively "fermentation/complex-API platform" (Concord, Laurus) plus "premium CDMO/API" (Divi's, Cohance).
- Concord is the closest listed comp. It is the only peer with comparable fermentation scale (1,250 KL vs 700 KL), but it de-rated after FY26 revenue fell 12% and EBITDA margin fell from about 42% to about 35%.
- Two names recur across many of the top-10 products and are the most direct competitors to Symbiotec's core corticosteroid business: **Zhejiang Xianju** (6 of 10 products) and **Tianjin Tianfa / Tianjin Tianyao** (6 of 10). **Pfizer CentreOne, Bayer, Curia and Axplora** are the main Western overlaps.
- The fall in the steroidal-hormone share from 44.2% (DRHP) to 23.8% (RHP) is most likely a definitional change, since the RHP excludes androstenedione and OHPC, rather than a real share loss. The analyst should treat the 44.2% figure as superseded.

### Gaps
- Neither the DRHP nor the RHP gives market shares for individual competitors; only Symbiotec's own shares are disclosed.
- I did not find the full F&S report (the RHP says it is on Symbiotec's website). I also did not reconcile the RHP EBITDA of 2,319.73 mn (26.59%) with broker EBITDA of Rs 237.9 cr (27.4%), which is likely a definitional difference.

## Q4. Who are the Indian fermentation-API peers, and who are the Indian steroid/hormone API peers?

### Takeaway
Indian fermentation-API capacity is thin. Concord (1,250 m3) is the clear leader. Symbiotec (700 KL), Laurus (240 KL) and Anthem (142 KL, rising to about 182 KL) follow, and Biocon is a long-standing fermentation player in statins and immunosuppressants. The PLI scheme has added commodity-antibiotic fermentation: Aurobindo/Lyfius pen-G at 15,000 MT, Kinvan clavulanic acid at 400 MT, and Orchid 7-ACA at 1,000 MT (delayed to Mar-2027). Indian steroid API peers are mostly **unlisted** (Coral Drugs, Mahima Life Sciences) or **captive divisions** of large pharma (Sun, Cipla, Lupin, Dr. Reddy's in testosterone). There is **no listed Indian steroid-API pure-play**.
